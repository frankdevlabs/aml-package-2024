### <a id="recitals"></a>Recitals

(1) Pursuant to Article 49(14), point (a) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, the terms of cooperation between permanent members and with observers are set out in this Regulation, thereby allowing uniformity of modalities of cooperation and avoiding the need of negotiation and conclusion of terms of cooperation by the participants to the college.

(2) The scope of this Regulation encompasses three distinct situations provided for in Article 49 of Directive (EU) 2024/1640. First, it covers the mandatory establishment of AML/CFT supervisory colleges pursuant to Article 49(1)(a) where a credit institution or financial institution, including groups thereof, has set up establishments in at least two Member States other than the Member State of its head office. Second, it covers mandatory colleges pursuant to Article 49(1)(b) where a third-country credit institution or financial institution has set up establishments in at least three Member States. Third, it covers colleges that may be established pursuant to Article 49(8) where a credit institution or financial institution established in the Union has set up establishments in at least two third countries.

(3) The functioning of AML/CFT colleges (hereinafter also referred as 'AML/CFT Supervisory Colleges') should be risk-based. The frequency and form of meetings, as well as the depth of information exchange, should be calibrated to the residual ML/TF risk profile of the institution or group concerned. This proportionality should allow for flexibility, including the possibility of carrying out college activities by written procedure, provided that doing so is documented and agreed among permanent members.

(4) The AML/CFT database should play a central role in facilitating information-sharing within AML/CFT colleges. Where information reported to AMLA through that database is relevant to the activities and objectives of a college, such information should, to the extent possible and subject to applicable confidentiality and data protection requirements, be made available to the members of that college. Given that the current use of multiple platforms for the exchange of documents within AML/CFT colleges entails several challenges, the AML/CFT database should also play a role to serve as a central repository and document-sharing platform for the purposes of the college framework.

(5) College meetings should be structured to promote effective information exchange and lead to clear, documented outcomes. To that end, agendas should be targeted and focused, with a clear statement of purpose and desired outcomes, and meeting materials should be circulated to participants sufficiently in advance. Where information requests are circulated ahead of the meeting, they should, to the greatest extent possible, align criteria and indicators so as to allow meaningful comparison of supervisory risk and data across cross-border establishments. Where the institution or group includes obliged entities active in specific sectors, the request shall incorporate indicators relevant to the specific ML/TF risks associated with those sectors. The lead supervisor shall ensure that all criteria and indicators are interpreted consistently across all college participants, thereby enabling a coherent and comparative assessment of risk data across cross-border establishments. Meetings should, where relevant, result in documented action points with clear ownership and deadlines.

(6) The identification of a parent undertaking as the reference entity for identifying the lead supervisor may result in a financial supervisor assuming the function of lead supervisor for the first time, in particular where the parent undertaking concerned has only recently become an obliged entity under the Regulation (EU) 2024/1624. In such cases, the financial supervisor concerned may not have previously been responsible for running the college and may therefore initially lack the necessary information. Similar difficulties may also arise in other duly justified cases, where the lead supervisor is unable to effectively run the college. In order to preserve the efficiency of college arrangements in such circumstances, it should be possible, in exceptional and duly justified cases, to provide for a transitional period during which another financial supervisor supports the lead supervisor in running the college. The appointment of a supporting supervisor should be subject to the agreement of the permanent members of the college, to ensure that the arrangement reflects a shared assessment of its necessity. Given its exceptional nature, that arrangement should be strictly time-limited and should, in any event, not exceed one year.

(7) Personal data, as defined in Article 4(1) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 may be needed to be processed for the purpose of establishing and maintaining an overview of the establishments of the credit or financial institution, including groups thereof and to carry out the tasks of the AML/CFT supervisory college. For that processing activity, as well as for the processing required for cooperation, the lead supervisor should be the data controller. If the exchange of information between college members entails exchange of personal data, each party should be considered as data controller for its own purposes.

(8) This Regulation is based on the draft regulatory technical standards submitted to the Commission by the Authority for Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism ('the Authority' or 'AMLA').

(9) The Authority has analysed the potential costs and benefits related to the draft regulatory technical standards on which this Regulation is based, and conducted an impact analysis.

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

## <a id="section-1"></a>SECTION 1

### <a id="article-1"></a>Article 1 — Subject matter and scope

This Regulation lays down the general conditions for the functioning, on a risk sensitive basis, of AML/CFT supervisory colleges established pursuant to Article 49 of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, in respect of credit institutions and financial institutions, including groups thereof, in the financial sector.

### <a id="article-2"></a>Article 2 — Definitions

For the purposes of this Regulation, in addition to the definitions set out in Article 2 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620, in Article 2 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 and Article 2 of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, the following definitions apply:

(1) 'lead supervisor' means the financial supervisor in charge of setting up the AML/CFT supervisory college pursuant to Article 49(1) and Article 49(8) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640.

(2) 'AML/CFT database' means the central AML/CFT database established and maintained by AMLA pursuant to Article 11 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620.

## <a id="section-2"></a>SECTION 2 — General conditions for the functioning of AML/CFT supervisory colleges in the financial sector

### <a id="article-3"></a>Article 3 — Information on the structure of the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof

1. The lead supervisor shall establish and maintain an up-to-date overview of entities identified in accordance with Article 49(5) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 for the purpose of identifying the permanent members and potential observers of an AML/CFT supervisory college, pursuant to Article 49 of Directive (EU) 2024/1640.

2. The overview pursuant to paragraph 1 shall contain at least the following information:

   (a) the names of all Member States and third countries in which entities identified in accordance with Article 49(5) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 have been established;
   (b) the name of the supervisor or supervisors of entities identified in accordance with point (a);
   (c) the name and, where available, the legal entity identifier (LEI) or other applicable identifier, of entities identified in accordance with point (a);
   (d) the level of ML/TF risk pursuant to Article 8 of this regulation associated with the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof and its cross-border establishments, to the extent known to the lead supervisor;
   (e) any relevant information on the ownership and group structure of the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof.

3. The information referred to in paragraph 2, points (a), (b) and (c) shall be displayed in a structured and comprehensible format having regard to the complexity of the entity or group structure and shall be made available to all permanent members and observers, in a secure manner.

4. The lead supervisor shall review and update the overview referred to in paragraph 1 regularly and whenever becoming aware of any relevant change(s) to the information collected pursuant to paragraph 2. For that purpose, the lead supervisor shall use the template set out in Annex I.

5. The overview pursuant to paragraph 1 and information on a specific AML/CFT supervisory college shall be shared with AMLA when the college is set up and thereafter, without undue delay, once the overview referred to in paragraph 1 has been updated pursuant to paragraph 4.

6. Permanent members and observers of an AML/CFT supervisory college shall exchange with the lead supervisor all necessary information to establish and maintain up to date the overview completed pursuant to paragraph 1.

### <a id="article-4"></a>Article 4 — List of permanent members and observers of the AML/CFT supervisory college

1. The lead supervisor shall establish and maintain an up-to-date list of all permanent members and observers of an AML/CFT supervisory college in accordance with the requirements for the establishment of the AML/CFT database. The list shall be accessible by all permanent members through the IT infrastructure that supports the AML/CFT database, and, upon request, be made available to observers. The list shall be reviewed at regular intervals and updated whenever a relevant change occurs.

2. The list pursuant to paragraph 1 shall contain, for each permanent member and observer, at least the following information:

   (a) the name of the authority;
   (b) the name and contact details of a generic point of contact within that authority and, in addition or alternatively, the contact details of the designated contact person(s);
   (c) an indication whether an authority is a permanent member or an observer and the applicable legal basis for the observer status pursuant to Article 49(3), (6), (7), (11) or (12) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640;

3. For permanent members and observers listed in the register of competent authorities established pursuant to Article 62 of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, the lead supervisor may rely on the information in that register. Where that register does not include the relevant contact details, the lead supervisor shall ensure those details are recorded in lists created in accordance with paragraph 1.

4. Permanent members and observers shall inform the lead supervisor without undue delay of any changes concerning information stipulated by paragraph 2.

### <a id="article-5"></a>Article 5 — Communication channels

1. Permanent members and observers of an AML/CFT college shall use secure communication channels for cooperation purposes, and where possible, the IT infrastructure that supports the AML/CFT database. College meetings shall be organised in a secure manner, with controlled access to the meeting room or online connection where relevant.

2. Permanent members shall agree on the language(s) to be used for communications within the AML/CFT supervisory college. Where information is not available in the language(s) so agreed, permanent members or observers sharing information shall provide it in one of those languages. Where this is not feasible, permanent members and observers sharing information shall strive to provide, at a minimum, a summary of that information in a language of the AML/CFT supervisory college.

### <a id="article-6"></a>Article 6 — Material developments and urgent information exchange

1. Where a college member or observer becomes aware of any fact that may have a negative impact on the fulfilment of requirements and supervisory expectations as stipulated by the applicable AML/CFT legal framework and that could affect other obliged entities, it shall inform all other college members and observers without undue delay.

2. Where a development referred to in paragraph 1 warrants the organisation of an ad hoc meeting, the college member or observer that identified the development may request that the lead supervisor convene such a meeting at short notice, setting out the reasons for the request. The lead supervisor shall organise the ad hoc meeting pursuant to Article 9 without undue delay.

3. Any information, including items listed in Article 17, that could significantly influence the assessment of the inherent or residual risk exposure to ML/TF of an obliged entity, including the information under Article 46(3) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 or that would warrant the discussion and agreement on a coordinated supervisory approach in accordance with Article 12 should be considered material for the purposes of this Regulation.

### <a id="article-7"></a>Article 7 — Mutual assistance

1. College members may request mutual assistance from each other in the performance of their AML/CFT supervisory tasks relating to the entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1). Mutual assistance may be requested, in particular, in the context of:

   (a) determination of the ML/TF risks related to all or some of entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1);
   (b) the outcome of relevant recent or planned supervisory activities;
   (c) assistance with on-site or off-site inspections carried out in the requesting authority's jurisdiction;
   (d) the assessment of the adequacy of the group-wide AML/CFT policies and procedures, the group-wide business-wide risk assessment, and the effectiveness of the group-wide compliance function;
   (e) any other information relevant to the performance of the AML/CFT supervisory tasks of the requesting authority in relation to the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof.

2. The requesting party shall submit its request to the requested party and inform the lead supervisor of this submission without undue delay. Requests for mutual assistance shall specify the information or assistance requested, the purpose for which it is sought, and the deadline foreseen which shall, except in duly justified cases, not be less than 10 working days. In exceptional circumstances where an oral request is made, it should be followed up without delay with a formal written request.

3. The supervisor receiving a request for mutual assistance shall respond within the deadline set out in the request. Where a college member is unable or not in a position to act on a request, it shall inform the requesting party and the lead supervisor of the related reasons, and shall, where possible, indicate alternative ways in which the assistance might be provided.

4. The lead supervisor shall be informed of the outcome of the request for mutual assistance and shall retain all requests it is informed of in a secure manner. The lead supervisor may inquire as to developments in the mutual assistance, and facilitate resolution where difficulties arise.

### <a id="article-8"></a>Article 8 — ML/TF risk assessment for AML/CFT supervisory college purposes

1. For the purpose of determining the frequency and form of college meetings pursuant to Article 9, the lead supervisor shall consider the ML/TF risk level of the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof on the basis of the risk assessments conducted pursuant to the applicable Union law, including in accordance with the regulatory technical standards adopted pursuant to Article 40(2) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 and pursuant to Article 12(7) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620, where available.

2. Where a group-level risk assessment is available pursuant to the applicable Union law, it shall serve as the default reference for college purposes. Where a group-level risk assessment is not available pursuant to the applicable Union law, the lead supervisor shall consider the individual ML/TF risk assessment of the cross-border establishments of the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof available from permanent members, in consultation with permanent members.

3. Regardless of the methodology used to determine the group-wide ML/TF risk, the final risk assessment shall result in one of the following risk categories: high, substantial, medium or low ML/TF risk.

4. For the purposes of paragraphs 1 and 2, the lead supervisor may also contact observers to obtain their input into the ML/TF risk assessment of the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof.

5. The risk determination made pursuant to this Article shall be reviewed by the lead supervisor, in consultation with permanent members and updated without undue delay where a material change in the ML/TF risk profile of the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof so warrants. The updated risk assessment shall be available to all permanent members and observers.

### <a id="article-9"></a>Article 9 — Frequency and form of college meetings

1. Where it is determined that the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof presents a high ML/TF risk pursuant to Article 8, the college shall hold at least one meeting per year. Where it is determined that the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof presents a substantial or medium ML/TF risk, the college shall hold at least one meeting every two years. Where it is determined that the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof presents a low ML/TF risk, the college may operate on the basis of written exchanges of information when deemed necessary, unless the lead supervisor considers that a meeting is warranted by one or several of the factors referred to in paragraph 3.

2. The form of the AML/CFT supervisory college meetings, including whether to hold physical meetings, virtual meetings, or to conduct college activities by written procedure, shall be determined by the lead supervisor, based on the specificities of the college and having regard to the efficiency of outcomes. This applies regardless of the ML/TF risk level of the institution or group. College activities conducted by written procedure shall be considered equivalent to a meeting for the purpose of satisfying the minimum frequency requirements in paragraph 1, provided that:

   (a) the decision to conduct activities by written procedure is documented and agreed among permanent members;
   (b) all permanent members are given adequate time to contribute; and
   (c) the outcome of the written procedure is documented and shared with all permanent members and observers.

3. The lead supervisor, shall determine the frequency and form of scheduled college meetings on a risk-sensitive basis, taking into account paragraphs 1 and 2 and the following factors:

   (a) the ML/TF risk associated with the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof and its establishments, as assessed in accordance with applicable Union law;
   (b) any significant change in that ML/TF risk since the previous college meeting;
   (c) the nature, scale, and complexity of the cross-border activities of credit institution or financial institution or group thereof;
   (d) the views expressed by permanent members;
   (e) the urgency of emerging ML/TF risks affecting the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof;
   (f) any recently crystallised ML/TF risks or serious breaches of AML/CFT requirements.

4. The lead supervisor may convene an ad hoc college meeting, with all or some of the college participants, either on its own initiative or upon request from a permanent member. This possibility shall notably be considered in case of a material development referred to in Article 6. The lead supervisor shall share those conclusions with the remaining college participants as relevant.

### <a id="article-10"></a>Article 10 — Object of college meetings

1. The lead supervisor shall ensure that each scheduled AML/CFT supervisory college meeting includes agenda items that reflect and are proportionate to the ML/TF risks of the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof, including where relevant:

   (a) the exchange of information on the ML/TF risk profile of the entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1), including updated individual risk assessments by permanent members;
   (b) early warnings of emerging ML/TF risks or vulnerabilities;
   (c) crystallised ML/TF risks and key supervisory findings relating to the AML/CFT policies, procedures and controls of entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1), including any provisional findings where serious breaches have been identified;
   (d) information on planned or recently completed AML/CFT supervisory actions, including on-site and off-site inspections;
   (e) sanctions or other administrative measures imposed, or under consideration, for breaches of AML/CFT obligations;
   (f) aggregated information on suspicious transaction and activity reports filed by the institution, such as on their quality or frequency, where available and permissible under applicable law;
   (g) discussion on whether common ML/TF risks or issues warrant a coordinated supervisory approach pursuant to Article 12 of this Regulation;
   (h) where relevant, a presentation of the group structure and business model and any possible material changes capable of impacting the level of ML/TF risk.

2. Each meeting agenda shall clearly state the purpose of the meeting and, where relevant, the expected outcomes. Meeting materials shall be circulated to all permanent members and relevant observers sufficiently ahead of the meeting to allow adequate preparation. In the case of ad hoc meetings, materials shall be circulated as early as practicable.

3. Each meeting shall result in a summary of conclusions indicating the key discussion points and, where relevant, action points. Action points shall identify the responsible authority and the applicable deadline for each item, to the extent possible. The summary of conclusions shall be circulated to all permanent members and relevant observers without undue delay following the meeting.

### <a id="article-11"></a>Article 11 — Risk-based approach to engagement in college activities

1. Permanent members and observers shall adopt a risk-based approach to participation in meetings of the AML/CFT supervisory college having regard, in particular, to the relevance of the meeting or the items on the agenda to their respective supervisory responsibilities, and to the ML/TF risk profile of the establishment(s) for which they are responsible.

2. In the event of a permanent member deciding not to attend the meeting, it shall inform the lead supervisor in advance of the meeting.

3. The lead supervisor shall ensure that permanent members and observers that do not participate in a particular college meeting have access to the relevant documentation and outcomes relating to that meeting.

4. Irrespective of their level of participation in college meetings pursuant to paragraph 1, permanent members and observers shall ensure a minimum level of active engagement with the college and shall, as a minimum, provide any information needed for the proper functioning of the AML/CFT supervisory college as requested by the lead supervisor.

5. Notwithstanding paragraph 1, the lead supervisor may formally invite a permanent member or observer that does not regularly participate in college meetings to participate in a specific meeting, where the lead supervisor considers that the participation of such member or observer is necessary for the effective functioning of the college.

6. Where one or several permanent members have decided not to attend a college meeting, pursuant to paragraph 2, the lead supervisor may decide to cancel the meeting or change the form of the college meeting, if no more than two permanent members, other than the lead supervisor, have confirmed their participation.

### <a id="article-12"></a>Article 12 — Coordinated supervisory approach

1. College members shall regularly discuss whether specific aspects of the AML/CFT supervision of entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1) could be more effectively addressed through a coordinated supervisory approach. This discussion shall result in a documented conclusion.

2. Permanent members shall agree on a coordinated supervisory approach which sets out the objectives and the related action(s) to ensure that entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1) comply with Union AML/CFT requirements and are supervised in a consistent manner across jurisdictions. A coordinated supervisory approach may include, inter alia, coordinated on-site or off-site inspections, coordinated thematic reviews, and may lead to enforcement or remediation measures.

3. Where a coordinated supervisory approach is proposed by the lead supervisor, any permanent member, AMLA or any other observer, such proposal shall be discussed at the next scheduled college meeting or in urgent cases via written procedure.

4. Before a coordinated supervisory approach is decided, the AML/CFT supervisory college shall assess:

   (a) the nature and level of the ML/TF risk that the coordinated supervisory approach is designed to assess or mitigate;
   (b) the objectives of the coordinated supervisory approach;
   (c) the types of supervisory actions to be undertaken;
   (d) relevant differences in the applicable legal and regulatory frameworks and how those differences are to be taken into account;
   (e) the supervisory resources required and their planned allocation.

5. To achieve the envisaged objectives, not all AML/CFT college members have to participate in the execution of an agreed coordinated supervisory approach, if justifiable. AML/CFT college members shall be updated in writing on the outcome of the coordinated supervisory approach taken.

6. Where a coordinated supervisory approach is agreed upon by the permanent members or observers taking part in it, the lead supervisor shall document and keep record of at least the following:

   (a) the participating members or observers;
   (b) the supervisory activities to be performed, including any joint supervisory action(s);
   (c) the timeline for implementation;
   (d) the modalities of information exchange; and
   (e) the options for coordinated follow-up, including, where applicable, enforcement measures.

   The participating permanent members and observers shall cooperate with the lead supervisor to provide the aforementioned information in a timely manner.

7. Where agreed measures have not produced the intended outcomes, the participating permanent members shall reassess the situation and consider the need for additional or more intrusive measures. This reassessment shall be documented.

8. Where a participating member fails to act in accordance with the agreed coordinated approach, any other member may refer the matter to the lead supervisor.

### <a id="article-13"></a>Article 13 — Cooperation with prudential supervisory colleges

1. Where a college of supervisors has been established for the same credit institution or financial institution or group thereof pursuant to Union law,[^f2] the lead supervisor shall cooperate with the authority responsible for coordinating that college for the purpose of ensuring coherent and efficient cross-college cooperation.

2. For the purposes of paragraph 1, the lead supervisor and the authority responsible for coordinating the prudential supervisory college shall:

   (a) exchange the information on college composition, including the overview referred to in Article 3;
   (b) engage on a regular basis to ensure that AML/CFT supervisory findings with potential prudential implications and prudential supervisory findings with potential ML/TF implications are shared in a timely manner between the respective colleges.

3. In preparation for AML/CFT supervisory college meetings or activities, the lead supervisor shall consider whether any topic to be discussed has potential prudential implications warranting prior information exchange with the authority responsible for coordinating the prudential college. Where this is the case, the lead supervisor shall inform that authority before the meeting or activity takes place.

4. Where the lead supervisor participates in a meeting of a supervisory college established pursuant to Union law, it shall share with the members of the AML/CFT supervisory college, without undue delay, any information relevant to the ML/TF risk profile of the entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1), subject to the confidentiality requirements applicable to that college.

[^f2]: See for instance Article 116 of Directive 2013/36/EU, Article 248 of Directive 2009/138/EC, Article 18 of Regulation (EU) No 648/2012, Article 119 of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114.

### <a id="article-14"></a>Article 14 — Confidentiality and permissible uses of information

1. Information exchanged within college activities shall be used by permanent members and observers solely for the purpose of performing their respective functions.

2. Any disclosure pursuant to this Article shall be limited to what is necessary and proportionate for the performance of the tasks of the recipient, and shall be supported by reasonable and justified grounds, having regard to the purpose for which the information was originally obtained.

3. Information exchanged within the AML/CFT supervisory college pursuant to this Regulation shall be disclosed to supervisors, including AMLA when acting as a supervisor, or public authorities as referred to in Article 52 of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 without prior consent of the originator of the information, except in those cases where the information originates from a third-country counterpart, in which case disclosure shall be subject to the terms of the written cooperation agreement referred to in Article 49(8) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 or, absent such agreement, to the prior consent of the third-country counterpart concerned.

   The originator of the information shall be notified of the following, to the extent possible before or at the latest at the time of disclosure:

   (a) the information shared;
   (b) the recipient of the information;
   (c) the rationale of the disclosure.

4. Information exchanged pursuant to this Regulation may be disclosed without prior consent of the originator of the information in other cases, where such disclosure is authorised or required under Union law, including in cases where the exchange of information is carried out with Financial Intelligence Units, competent authorities referred to in Article 2(1)(44), points (c) and (d) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624, with AMLA pursuant to Article 31(1)(d) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 or with authorities in charge of implementing targeted financial sanctions mentioned in Article 66 of Directive (EU) 2024/1640. The same applies when the information received by the supervisor is used for the purposes set out in Article 67(3) points (b) and (c) of the Directive (EU) 2024/1640.

   The originator of the information shall be notified of the following, to the extent possible before or at the latest at the time of disclosure:

   (a) the information shared;
   (b) the recipient of the information;
   (c) the legal basis of the disclosure;
   (d) the rationale of the disclosure.

### <a id="article-15"></a>Article 15 — Management of disputes

1. Where the financial supervisors responsible for the AML/CFT supervision of entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1) are unable to reach a common understanding on the identification of the authority that should act as the lead supervisor for the purposes of establishing the AML/CFT supervisory college, any of those supervisors may refer the matter to AMLA in accordance with Articles 31 and 33 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620.

2. Where a dispute arises between permanent members or with observers in relation to the functioning of the AML/CFT supervisory college or the interpretation or implementation of the written cooperation and information-sharing agreement, and the parties are unable to resolve the dispute among themselves, any party may refer the matter to AMLA in accordance with Articles 31 and 33 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620.

## <a id="section-3"></a>SECTION 3 — Terms of cooperation between permanent members of the AML/CFT supervisory college

### <a id="article-16"></a>Article 16 — Invitation of permanent members to participate in the AML/CFT supervisory college

1. The lead supervisor shall contact financial supervisors specified pursuant to Article 49(2) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, who shall act as permanent members, to set up the AML/CFT supervisory college.

2. A financial supervisor that knows, or becomes aware, that it does not fulfil, or will cease to fulfil, the conditions to be permanent member of an AML/CFT supervisory college pursuant to Article 49(2) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 shall inform the lead supervisor without undue delay.

3. The lead supervisor shall without undue delay:

   (a) provide any new permanent member of an AML/CFT supervisory college with all information needed to fully participate in the work of the college;
   (b) inform all existing permanent members and observers of changes related to the status of college membership;
   (c) update the list referred to in Article 4.

4. The lead supervisor and permanent members shall ensure that the representatives participating in college meetings and activities have sufficient knowledge of and ongoing supervisory involvement with the credit and financial institution or group thereof, taking into account the topics to be discussed and the objectives to be pursued. Representatives shall have the power to commit their authority as permanent member, to the maximum extent possible under their applicable institutional framework.

### <a id="article-17"></a>Article 17 — Information exchange between permanent members

1. Permanent members shall exchange, at a minimum, any information relevant for the supervision of the credit institution or financial institution or group, including:

   (a) information on the entity or group's legal, governance and organisational structure, covering all entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1);
   (b) individual risk assessments of the entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1);
   (c) relevant information on the beneficial owners and senior management, including outcomes of fit and proper checks;
   (d) relevant policies, procedures and controls that apply to entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1);
   (e) relevant information on customer due diligence matters, adequately aggregated or anonymised where relevant and possible;
   (f) information referred to in Article 23(1), point (a) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 concerning the quality and quantity of suspicious transaction reports filed by the institution and its cross-border establishments, where available and permissible under applicable law without prejudice to Article 46 (3) (d) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640;
   (g) adverse developments in relation to entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1), which could seriously affect other entity or group;
   (h) results of AML/CFT supervisory actions, including on-site and off-site inspections;
   (i) pecuniary sanctions that financial supervisors intend to impose and administrative measures that financial supervisors intend to apply.

2. Where the information referred to in paragraph 1 is already available in the AML/CFT database, AMLA shall ensure that such information is disseminated to the members of the AML/CFT supervisory college.

3. Information shall be exchanged on a regular basis in preparation for college meetings and on an ad hoc basis where urgency requires, notably in case of material development pursuant to Article 6.

## <a id="section-4"></a>SECTION 4 — Terms of cooperation with observers of the AML/CFT supervisory college

### <a id="article-18"></a>Article 18 — Participation of observers from Member States

1. The lead supervisor shall identify and in compliance with provisions of Article 49(6), (7) and (12) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 invite financial supervisors, non-financial supervisors and other entities from Member States to participate in the AML/CFT supervisory college as observers. In compliance with Article 49(10) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, the lead supervisor shall always invite AMLA to participate in the AML/CFT supervisory college.

2. Before inviting an observer pursuant to Article 49(6), (7) or (12) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, the lead supervisor shall:

   (a) consult with all permanent members and take into account any observations or objections received within a reasonable deadline to be set by the lead supervisor, which should to the extent feasible be no less than ten working days;
   (b) ensure that the proposed observer accepts the applicable terms of participation in accordance with this Regulation, and it is subject to professional secrecy requirements stipulated by Directive (EU) 2024/1640.

3. Permanent members of the college shall raise any observations or objections to the proposed observer(s) referred to in paragraph 2 in writing, with a reasoned explanation, within the deadline set by the lead supervisor, which should in any event be no less than ten working days.

4. The lead supervisor may invite an observer where no permanent member has raised an objection within the deadline set by the lead supervisor. Where a minority of permanent members object to the participation of such observer, the lead supervisor may, in agreement with a majority of permanent members, propose the organisation of dedicated meetings or information exchanges with the relevant observer. No information obtained from the supervisors objecting an observer referred to in paragraph 2 shall be shared in these meetings or information exchanges.

5. When a new observer referred to in paragraph 2 joins the college, the lead supervisor shall update the list referred to in Article 4 of this Regulation and shall inform all permanent members and observers of the college. The lead supervisor shall proceed similarly when an observer is no longer part of the college.

6. Where the college considers that the specific circumstances of a given observer warrant more detailed or tailored arrangements beyond the provisions of this Regulation, the college may conclude a written agreement with that observer specifying additional terms. Such an agreement shall be consistent with the provisions of this Regulation and shall be recorded by the lead supervisor and made available to all permanent members of the college upon request. Terms of participation shall be signed by the relevant observer prior to its first participation in college activities.

7. The status, rights and obligations of AMLA as an observer of an AML/CFT supervisory college are specified by Article 49(10) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 and Article 31 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620. Provisions of this Regulation apply to AMLA only if they do not restrict status, rights and obligations of AMLA as stipulated by Union law.

### <a id="article-19"></a>Article 19 — Information exchange with observers

1. Observers shall have access to the information exchanged within the college that is relevant to the performance of their functions in relation to the entities identified in accordance with Article 3(1). The scope of such information is to be determined by the lead supervisor, after consultation with the relevant observer and permanent members. Before an observer participates in a college meeting for the first time, the lead supervisor shall confirm in writing to that observer the scope of its participation and the applicable confidentiality conditions.

2. Observers shall treat all information received in the context of their participation in the college as confidential and shall not use it for purposes other than those set out in this Regulation and Union law.

3. The lead supervisor may, in agreement with permanent members, restrict an observer's access to specific categories of information where necessary to protect the confidentiality of information for reasons specified by Union law.

### <a id="article-20"></a>Article 20 — Participation of observers in the activities of the AML/CFT supervisory college

1. Observers may participate in the meetings and activities of the college to the extent that such meetings and activities are relevant to their functions. Observers shall be invited by the lead supervisor, after consultation with the permanent members, to meeting(s) of the AML/CFT supervisory college.

2. Observers may request and receive mutual assistance in accordance with Article 7 of this Regulation, where this is necessary for the performance of their tasks.

3. The participation of an observer may be suspended or withdrawn by the lead supervisor, after consultation with the permanent members, where:

   (a) the observer no longer meets the conditions for participation set out in this Regulation or Union law;
   (b) the observer has failed to comply with its confidentiality obligations; or
   (c) the lead supervisor and permanent members determine that the continued participation of the observer is no longer warranted.

4. The observer shall be informed of any proposed suspension or withdrawal within a reasonable period of time before any decision takes effect.

## <a id="section-5"></a>SECTION 5 — Conditions for the participation of third-country financial supervisors

### <a id="article-21"></a>Article 21 — Participation of third-country observers

1. Third-country supervisory authorities may participate in a college as observers only where the conditions set out in Article 49(11) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 are fulfilled and the relevant authority has signed the written cooperation agreement referred to in Article 23 of this Regulation.

2. The lead supervisor shall maintain a record of all signed written cooperation agreements with third-country supervisors and make them available to AMLA and permanent members upon request.

### <a id="article-22"></a>Article 22 — Assessment of conditions for participation of third-country observers

1. Before inviting a third-country counterpart to participate in the college as an observer or agreeing to a request for participation from a third country observer, the lead supervisor shall conduct an assessment of whether the requirements set out in Article 49(11) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 are fulfilled.

2. The lead supervisor, when carrying out an assessment pursuant to Article 49(11) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, in order to be in the position to decide on the participation of a third-country observer in an AML/CFT supervisory college, shall take into account, in particular the following:

   (a) relevant assessments or mutual recognition agreements adopted at Union level;
   (b) relevant assessments or mutual recognition agreements adopted by other supervisors.

3. Where the outcome of the lead supervisor's assessment diverges from positions adopted by other AML/CFT colleges concerning the same third country counterpart, the lead supervisor shall engage in dialogue with those lead supervisors and, where necessary, with AMLA, to reach a consistent position before concluding the assessment.

4. The lead supervisor shall share the assessment and its outcome with all permanent members, and with AMLA. The assessment document shared shall include a clear statement of the outcome and the rationale supporting it, including the sources of information and criteria relied upon. Where the third-country counterpart is assessed as not meeting the requirements set out in Article 49(11) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, it shall not participate in the AML/CFT supervisory college as an observer.

5. AMLA will make available a list of the third-country authorities that have been assessed on their compliance with the requirements set out in Article 49(11) of the Directive (EU) 2024/1640, specifying for each authority the lead supervisor responsible for the assessment and the outcome thereof.

### <a id="article-23"></a>Article 23 — Written cooperation and information-sharing agreement with counterparts in third countries

1. A written cooperation and information-sharing agreement with a third country counterpart shall be established, when:

   (a) the outcome of the assessment referred to in Article 22 allows for the third country counterpart to participate in the AML/CFT supervisory college as an observer;
   (b) permanent members of the AML/CFT supervisory college agree with the participation of a third country counterpart in that college. Such agreement shall be recorded and securely stored by the lead supervisor and made available upon request to AMLA, and to any permanent member or observer of the AML/CFT supervisory college.

2. The lead supervisor shall use the template set out in Annex II to this Regulation for the written cooperation and information-sharing agreement with the third country counterpart. Deviations from the template may only be made where strictly necessary to accommodate constraints arising from the third-country counterpart's applicable law or institutional requirements. Where the cooperation and information sharing agreement cannot be concluded because the third-country counterpart requests modifications to the template that cannot be accommodated, the lead supervisor may request AMLA's support to facilitate the conclusion of the agreement.

3. The lead supervisor shall verify that the third country counterpart endorses and signs the cooperation and information-sharing agreement prior to their participation in the college meetings and activities as observers. The lead supervisor shall agree with the third-country counterpart the level of seniority required for the signatories of the cooperation and information-sharing agreement, having regard to the nature and significance of the cooperation envisaged. The lead supervisor shall ensure that the signing and validation process is secure and preserves the integrity and authenticity of the signed agreement.

## <a id="section-6"></a>SECTION 6 — Additional measures

### <a id="article-24"></a>Article 24 — Invited participants

1. The lead supervisor, in agreement with the permanent members, may invite the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof for which the AML/CFT supervisory college is established to participate in a dedicated part of a college meeting. Participation of the credit institution or financial institution or group thereof shall be limited to a dedicated slot of the meeting, separate from the parts of the meeting reserved to permanent members and observers.

2. The lead supervisor, in agreement with the permanent members, may invite a supervisor of a third country to participate in a college meeting as an invited participant, where the conditions for its participation as an observer pursuant to Article 21 are not met because the equivalence assessment referred to in Article 49(11) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 could not be conducted. This paragraph shall not apply where the equivalence assessment referred to in Article 49(11) has been carried out and has concluded that the third-country counterpart does not meet the conditions set out in Article 49(11) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640.

3. Where a third-country supervisor participates in a college meeting pursuant to paragraph 2, the lead supervisor, in agreement with the permanent members, shall:

   (a) ensure that the information that is subject to confidentiality rules is not shared;
   (b) determine the parts of the meeting to which that supervisor may be invited;
   (c) identify any other condition necessary to safeguard the confidentiality of the information exchanged within the college.

## <a id="section-7"></a>SECTION 7 — Final provisions

### <a id="article-25"></a>Article 25 — Transitional provisions for existing AML/CFT supervisory colleges

1. AML/CFT supervisory colleges established prior to the date of entry into force of this Regulation shall continue to operate, and shall not be required to be re-established, provided that they are brought into conformity with the requirements of this Regulation in accordance with paragraph 2.

2. Written arrangements, agreements, terms of participation of observers and other documentation governing the functioning of a college and adopted prior to the date of entry into force of this Regulation shall continue to apply to the extent that they are compatible with this Regulation. No re-signing of any existing agreements or documentation by existing permanent members is required upon accession of a new permanent member. New permanent members accede to the college framework by acknowledging receipt of the relevant college documentation and of their invitation, pursuant to this Regulation.

3. The lead supervisor shall, in consultation with the permanent members, review the existing written arrangements, agreements, and other documentation referred to in paragraph 2 and shall update them as necessary to ensure full conformity with the requirements of this Regulation.

### <a id="article-26"></a>Article 26 — Exceptional transitional arrangements applicable to a newly appointed lead supervisor

1. Where the lead supervisor identified has not previously performed the function of lead supervisor for the college concerned, because the parent undertaking became an obliged entity as a result of the application of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624, a transitional period may be applied, in exceptional and duly justified circumstances.

2. A transitional period may also be applied, in exceptional and duly justified circumstances, in other cases where the lead supervisor identified is not in a position to effectively run the college.

3. During the transitional period referred to in paragraph 1 or 2, another financial supervisor shall be appointed to support the lead supervisor in running the college. The appointment of the supporting supervisor shall be subject to the agreement of the permanent members of the college.

4. The supporting supervisor shall, where applicable and unless otherwise agreed by the permanent members:

   (a) in the case referred to in paragraph 1, be the financial supervisor that performed the function of lead supervisor for the college immediately prior to the parent undertaking becoming an obliged entity;
   (b) in the cases referred to in paragraph 2, be the financial supervisor that performed the function of lead supervisor for the college immediately prior to the circumstances giving rise to the transitional period.

5. The transitional period referred to in paragraph 1 or 2 shall not exceed one year.

### <a id="article-27"></a>Article 27 — Entry into force and application

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

It shall apply from 10 July 2027 (it cannot apply before the transposition date of the AMLD – if the COM adopts this Regulation after this date, then this provision can be deleted).

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels,

For the Commission
The President

2.2 In case non-permanent members or non-observers are invited to a specific College meeting as invited participants as set out in the Regulation, the Lead Supervisor will notify the Observer of this prior to the meeting. 2.3 The Lead Supervisor will make available to the Observer all relevant information prior to the College meeting or at the same time as such information is made available to the permanent members and other observers.

2.4 The Observer will ensure that the most appropriate representatives of the Observer participate in the College meetings and other activities, based on their knowledge of the credit and financial institution or group thereof and its' involvement in ongoing supervisory activities.

**3. Mutual assistance in the College**

3.1 Pursuant to Article 7 of the Regulation, the Observer may request mutual assistance, including supervisory cooperation and the exchange of information, from permanent members or other observers of the College in any matters relevant to the AML/CFT supervision of the Obliged Entity and at least in matters dealt with in the College.

3.2 Permanent members and other observers of the College may also request mutual assistance, including supervisory cooperation and the exchange of information, from the Observer in the same matters.

3.3 Where a request for mutual assistance is made, the process described in Article 7 of the Regulation should be followed, including the following:

   3.3.1. The requesting party shall submit its request to the requested party and inform the Lead supervisor without undue delay following this submission. Requests for mutual assistance shall specify the information or assistance requested, the purpose for which it is sought, and the deadline foreseen which shall, except in cases duly justified, not be less than 10 working days. In exceptional circumstances where an oral request is made, it should be followed up without delay with a formal written request.

   3.3.2 The supervisor receiving a request for mutual assistance shall respond within the deadline specified in the request. Where a permanent member is unable or not in a position to act on a request, it shall inform the requesting party of the related reasons, and shall, where possible, indicate alternative ways in which the assistance might be provided.

   3.3.3 Where information is not available in the language(s) of the AML/CFT supervisory college, the requested party should consider providing a summary in a language of the AML/CFT supervisory college.

**4. Confidentiality and use of information exchanged in the College**

4.1 Pursuant to Article 14 of the Regulation, permanent members will:

   4.1.1 Treat all non-public information received from the Observer as part of the College as confidential and process the personal data received in compliance with applicable data protection rules; and

   4.1.2 Disclose such non-public information to parties other than permanent members and observers of the College only after having obtained prior consent from the Observer.

   4.1.3 Use such non-public information for the purposes specified in the Regulation, namely:

       4.1.3.1 To ensure that the Obliged Entity complies with the requirements stemming from Directive (EU) 2024/1640;

       4.1.3.2 To inform their assessment of the ML/TF risks to which the Obliged Entity is exposed.

   4.1.4 Use such non-public information for purposes other than those specified in Paragraph 4.1.3 of the Agreement only after having obtained prior consent from the Observer.

   4.1.5 Verify that other observers joining the college are bound by confidentiality regimes equivalent to those applying to the college members.

4.2 The Observer will:

   4.2.1 treat all non-public information obtained as part of the College as confidential and process the personal data received in accordance with Union data protection rules ensuring an adequate level of protection applicable to the Observer; and

   4.2.2 where the disclosure of such non-public information to parties other than permanent members and observers of the College is permissible under the applicable law, it may be disclosed only after having obtained prior consent from the permanent members or observers from which the information originated or that may be affected by the information disclosure.

   4.2.3 use the information received only to inform its assessment of the ML/TF risks to which the Obliged Entity is exposed and/or for the purpose of conducting its supervisory activities.

4.3 For the avoidance of doubt, 'non-public information' within the meaning of Paragraph 4 of the Agreement includes requests for mutual assistance.

4.4 The Lead Supervisor will ensure that all persons dealing with or having access to non-public information shared in the context of the College are bound by obligations of professional or official secrecy. In particular, the Lead Supervisor will ensure that other observers are bound by the same requirements as those which apply to the Observer pursuant to Article 4 of this Agreement.

**5. Final provisions**

5.1 The Lead Supervisor will inform the Observer of any material update made to the Agreement. Should the Observer disagree with an update, the Observer may terminate the Agreement in accordance with Paragraph 5.6 below.

5.2 The Agreement will not be published by the Observer, the Lead Supervisor, any other permanent member or observers of the College.

5.3 Non-public information exchanged within the College should be treated as confidential also after the termination of this Agreement, or after an authority or a body ceases to be a permanent member or an observer within the College.

5.4 The Lead Supervisor signs the Agreement on behalf of all permanent members.

5.5 The Agreement can be modified only upon mutual consent of the Lead Supervisor and the Observer.

5.6 Either party may terminate the Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party.

5.7 The Terms apply from the date on which the Lead Supervisor and the Observer have both signed the Terms.
