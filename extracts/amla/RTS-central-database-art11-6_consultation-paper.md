THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 31 May 2024 establishing the Authority for Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism and amending Regulations (EU) No 1093/2010, (EU) No 1094/2010 and (EU) No 1095/2010,[^1] and in particular Article 11(6) thereof,

[^1]: OJ L, 2024/1620, 19.6.2024.

### <a id="recitals"></a>Recitals

(1) Supervisory data is essential for an effective risk-based approach to AML/CFT supervision. To that end, the Authority should establish a central AML/CFT database to support targeted, risk-based supervision and oversight of the financial and non-financial sector, identify systemic weaknesses and emerging risks and contribute to the assessment and strategic analyses of ML/TF threats, risks and methods.

(2) In order to ensure proportionality and efficiency in supervisory reporting, this Regulation takes into account the principles set out in Regulation 2025/2088, which aim to streamline reporting requirements, reduce unnecessary administrative burden, and enhance the usability and consistency of reported data. In particular, this Regulation seeks to minimise duplicative and ad hoc reporting requests by establishing clear and harmonised reporting standards. Given the highly technical nature of data exchange specifications and the need to ensure their timely adaptation to technological developments, these RTS should not prescribe detailed technical provisions on the means for transmitting information to the central AML/CFT database.

(3) To support the Authority in the performance of its tasks under point (a) of article 5(3) and point (a) of article 5(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620, the central database should contain, for each Member State, a register of supervisory authorities and self-regulatory bodies their mandate, tasks and powers and, where applicable, information on the leading supervisor or the representative of the coordination mechanism. Information related to self-regulatory bodies and the obliged entities they supervise should be communicated to the database by the public body overseeing those self-regulatory bodies.

(4) To ensure that the Authority can appropriately perform its tasks under point (a) and (f) of Article 5(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620, supervisory authorities, as defined in article 2(1)(46) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624, should transmit to the central AML/CFT database not only the outcomes of their assessments of the inherent risk, quality of control and residual risk profiles of all credit institutions and financial institutions that meet the criteria set out in Article 12(1), but also the data points collected from obliged entities for the purposes of assessing and classifying their inherent risk profile, quality of controls and residual risk profile, as established under Article 40(2) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 and where applicable, information on group affiliation and AML/CFT supervisory colleges, in order to facilitate cross-border cooperation and support coherent oversight. The Authority should adopt the risk-based and proportionate approach of the [RTS under art. 40(2) Directive (EU) 2024/1640], which takes into account differences in terms of the scope of the information requested and timelines for updates to the assessment based on the entities sector, size and nature of the business and the level of risk.

(5) To ensure that the information contained in the central AML/CFT database allows the Authority to properly perform its tasks, in particular, under point (c) of Article 5(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620, supervisory authorities should transmit information on all administrative measures, pecuniary sanctions and periodic penalty payments relating to breaches and weaknesses.

(6) Supervisory authorities should transmit to the central AML/CFT database information on opinions or advice related to ML/TF risks that they provide to other authorities in the context of authorisation procedures, withdrawal of authorisation procedures, and 'fit and proper' assessments of shareholders or members of the management body of individual obliged entities where they raise concerns from an ML/TF perspective or constitute a negative assessment.

(7) The reports of thematic reviews, as well as information from other types of supervisory activities, including their nature and scope shall be transmitted to the central AML/CFT database. This information enables the Authority to maintain a comprehensive and up-to-date overview on the intensity and focus of AML/CFT supervision across Member States and contributes to effective indirect supervision and oversight, and enable AMLA to perform its tasks under point (e) of Article 5(3) and point (e) of Article 5(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620.

(8) Requests for additional information pursuant to Article 11(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 should remain proportionate to the objective pursued and should not impose any undue administrative burden on supervisory authorities. Such requests should be made only where the information sought is reasonably necessary for the performance of the Authority's tasks as set out in Article 5 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620.

(9) The exchange of information and cooperation among relevant actors involved in the application of the AML/CFT framework should be subject to confidentiality rules accompanied by an obligation to obtain the prior consent of the originating authority before any disclosure outside the AML/CFT supervisory system, except where Union law provides otherwise.

(10) All the personal data processed for the implementation of this Regulation should be handled in accordance with the data protection framework of the Union, including the principles relating to the processing such as lawfulness, fairness and transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity and confidentiality, and accountability.

(11) Data protection laws, in particular Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council and Regulation (EU) 2018/1725 of the European Parliament and of the Council are applicable to the processing of personal data.

(12) The European Data Protection Supervisor was consulted in accordance with Article 42(1) of Regulation (EU) 2018/1725 and provided formal comments on [date].

(13) This Regulation is based on the draft regulatory technical standards submitted to the Commission by the Authority.

(14) The Authority has analysed the potential costs and benefits related to the draft regulatory technical standards on which this Regulation is based, and conducted an impact analysis.

(15) As the requirements stipulated by Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 apply to football clubs and football agents from 10 July 2029, this Regulation provides for a deferred application date in relation to these obliged entities.

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

### <a id="article-1"></a>Article 1 — Information on supervisory authorities, self-regulatory bodies and their areas of responsibility

1. For the purposes of Article 11(2), point (a), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620, supervisory authorities shall transmit the following information to the central AML/CFT database:
   - (a) the name of their Member State;
   - (b) the name and contact information of the supervisory authority;
   - (c) the name, position and contact information of the persons designated by the authority to grant access to the database to the authority's staff;
   - (d) where applicable, name and contact information of the relevant self-regulatory body/bodies the supervisory authority oversees;
   - (e) where applicable, name and contact information of the leading supervisor in accordance with Article 37(4), first subparagraph of Directive (EU) 2024/1640;
   - (f) where applicable, name and contact information of the representative of the coordination mechanism in accordance with Article 37(4), second subparagraph, point (b), of Directive (EU) 2024/1640;
   - (g) where applicable, which categories of obliged entities listed in Article 3 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 are subject to their supervision, as well as the number of supervised obliged entities for each category;
   - (h) where applicable, which categories of obliged entities listed in Article 3 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 are subject to supervision by self-regulatory bodies in compliance with Article 37(3) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, as well as the number of supervised obliged entities for each category;
   - (i) where applicable, which public authority pursuant to Article 52(1) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 oversees respective self-regulatory bodies designated pursuant to Article 37(3) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640;
   - (j) the level of risk applicable to the sector or sectors under supervision by the reporting supervisory authority according to the most recent national risk assessment conducted pursuant to Article 8(6) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, specifying the date and period of assessment covered in the assessment or, for the Authority, the most recent risk assessment at Union level;
   - (k) the tasks of the supervisory authority in accordance with Article 37(5) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640;
   - (l) the powers of the supervisory authority in accordance with Article 37(6) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640;
   - (m) where applicable, the powers of the supervisory authority in accordance with Article 37(7) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640.
2. The information referred to in this Article shall be transmitted to the Authority for the first time by 15 July 2027 with the exception of the data under point 1(h), for which the first submission date shall be 31 March 2028, reflecting the number of obliged entities as of 31 December 2027. The number of supervised obliged entities per category under point 1 (g) of this article submitted by 15 July 2027 shall reflect the number of obliged entities as of 31 December 2026.
3. In the case of the supervisory authorities in the non-financial sector and public authorities overseeing self-regulatory bodies, the transmission of information until 27 June 2028 will be done on a voluntary basis, as per Article 106(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620.
4. Any change in the information shall be recorded by the supervisory authority in the central database no later than 30 working days after the change becomes effective, with the exception of the numbers of supervised obliged entities per category under point 1 (g) and (h) of this article, for which the data shall be recorded by the supervisory authority in the central database by 31 March of each year, reflecting the number of obliged entities as of 31 December of the previous year.

### <a id="article-2"></a>Article 2 — Information on obliged entities to be submitted by supervisory authorities

1. To fulfil their obligations under Article 11(2), point (b) and (e), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620, supervisory authorities shall transmit to the central database the following information in respect of each obliged entity under their supervision or under the supervision of the self-regulatory bodies they oversee:
   - (a) reference of the obliged entity. For legal persons, the name, address, national registration number and, where available, LEI; and, for obliged entities that are natural persons in the financial sector, the name, surname and address;
   - (b) where available, the inherent risk profile of the obliged entity, as determined in accordance with Article [XX] of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 202../xxx, as well as the documentation of any adjustments performed where the inherent risk score does not adequately reflect the level of ML/TF risks to which the obliged entity is exposed;
   - (c) where available, the quality of the obliged entity's AML/CFT controls, as assessed in accordance with Article [XX] of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 202../xxx, as well as the documentation of any adjustments performed where the combined score per category does not adequately reflect the level of quality of controls of the obliged entity;
   - (d) where available, the residual risk profile of the obliged entity, as determined in accordance with Article 4 Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 202../xxx ;
   - (e) where available, the data points on the basis of which the obliged entity's inherent risk profile and the quality of its AML/CFT controls, as referred to respectively in points b. and c., have been determined and assessed;
   - (f) whether there are central contact points as referred to in Article 41 of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, and their identification and Member State. This information should be provided by the host supervisor.
2. For the purposes of point e. of the first paragraph of this article, supervisory authorities shall refer to Annex I, Sections A and B, of the RTS under Article 40(2) of [Directive (EU) 2024/1640], by directly transmitting the data points related to the obliged entity.
3. Where the obliged entity forms part of a group within the meaning of Article 2(1), point (41), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624, the supervisory authority in charge of the parent undertaking within the meaning of Article 2(1), point (42), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624, shall also include the name and country of establishment of the parent undertaking within the meaning of Article 2(1), point (42), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624;
4. Where an AML/CFT supervisory college has been established in relation to the obliged entity according to Article 49 or Article 50 of Directive (EU) 2024/1640, the lead supervisor of the college shall also transmit, where applicable, the name, role and country of establishment of permanent members and onboarded observers.
5. The information referred to in this Article shall be transmitted to the Authority by 31 May and should refer to the period between 1 January and 31 December of the previous year. For the financial sector, the information shall be transmitted to the Authority for the first time by 31 May 2028. For the non-financial sector, the date shall be 31 May 2031.
6. For the purpose of paragraph 1, points b., c., d., and e., and of paragraph 2, the information shall be transmitted to the database no later than 10 working days following the date set in article 5 [RTS 40(2) FS and NFS] by supervisory authorities in accordance with the frequency set out in Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 202../xxx . In relation to the adjustments to the inherent risk score or the controls quality scores that took place during the course of the year, the adjusted scores, as well as the supporting documentation of the adjustments, should be submitted to the database within 30 working days of the adjustment taking place.

### <a id="article-3"></a>Article 3 — Information on administrative measures, pecuniary sanctions and periodic penalty payments

1. For the purposes of Article 11(2), point (c), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620, supervisory authorities shall transmit to the central AML/CFT database information on administrative measures, pecuniary sanctions and periodic penalty payments imposed in the course of supervision of individual obliged entities in response to breaches of AML/CFT requirements and weaknesses in the internal policies, procedures and controls of the obliged entity that are likely to result in breaches.
2. Where this information has not already been submitted pursuant to Article 2 of this Delegated Regulation, supervisory authorities shall transmit to the central AML/CFT database all available information related to the obliged entity. Specific information about the administrative measures applied, pecuniary sanctions and periodic penalty payments imposed by supervisory authorities shall be transmitted to the central AML/CFT database including:
   - (a) a reference to the obliged entity to which the administrative measure, pecuniary sanction or periodic penalty payment applies;
   - (b) the date of the imposition of the administrative measure, pecuniary sanction or periodic penalty payment;
   - (c) the type of administrative measure;
   - (d) where deemed appropriate by the reporting supervisory authority, additional details of the administrative measure, pecuniary sanction or periodic penalty payment not already reported under any of the other points of this paragraph;
   - (e) whether the administrative measure or pecuniary sanction is linked to a natural person specifying the function that the natural person is holding in the obliged entity, the reasons explaining the link and the following information regarding the concerned natural person: name, surname, date of birth, nationality and country of residence;
   - (f) the status of the administrative measure, pecuniary sanction or periodic penalty payment, including whether any appeal has been brought against them, as well as whether any settlement has been reached between the supervisor and the obliged entity;
   - (g) the date, where available and applicable, in which:
     - the administrative measure is to be complied with;
     - the payment of the pecuniary sanction is due;
     - the effect of the administrative measure ceases;
     - the periodic penalty payment ends;
   - (h) the amount of the pecuniary sanction or periodic penalty payment. In the case of settlements, both the imposed amount, when reporting the initial imposition of the sanction, and the settled amount once that amount is known;
   - (i) whether and how the administrative measure, pecuniary sanction or periodic penalty payment has been published, including the reasons for any anonymous publication, delay in publication or non-publication and a link to the publication, where available;
3. Specific information about the nature of the breaches and weaknesses and the supervisory activities and outcomes that led to the administrative measure being applied or the pecuniary sanction or periodic penalty payment being imposed that supervisory authorities shall transmit to the central AML/CFT database include:
   - (a) a reference to the supervisory activity through which the breach or weakness has been detected;
   - (b) the area or areas of AML/CFT compliance to which the breach or weakness concerns;
   - (c) a description of the breach or weakness;
   - (d) the level of gravity of the breach
   - (e) the status of the breach or weakness, including whether it has been remediated or is ongoing;
   - (f) indication on whether the breach or weakness only concerns the obliged entity and its local branches, agents or distributors or it also has any cross-border impact;
   - (g) indication of whether any follow-up actions are planned by the supervisor in response to the breach or weakness;
4. For the financial sector, the information referred to in this Article shall be transmitted to the Authority for the first time by 15 July 2027, concerning information on administrative measures, pecuniary sanctions and periodic penalty payments imposed after the specified date. For the non-financial sector, the date shall be 31 December 2029.
5. The information referred to in this Article shall be transmitted in relation to all administrative measures, pecuniary sanctions and periodic penalty payments imposed. The transmission shall take place without undue delay, and, in any case, no later than 30 working days delay following the imposition of the measure or pecuniary sanction and shall be updated without undue delay, and, in any case, no later than 30 working days, following any change to any of the reported information.

### <a id="article-4"></a>Article 4 — Information on advice or opinions provided to other authorities

1. Where an advice or opinion provided pursuant to Article 11(2), point (d), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 - whether released by the supervisory authority on its own initiative or in response to a request from another authority - raises concerns from an ML/TF perspective or constitutes a negative assessment, supervisory authorities shall transmit to the central AML/CFT database the following information:
   - (a) a summary of the request, where relevant;
   - (b) a summary of the ML/TF aspects of the advice or opinion provided, including the outcome and the ML/TF concerns raised;
   - (c) the type of the advice or opinion;
   - (d) the name and country of the receiving authority;
   - (e) the date the advice or opinion was provided;
   - (f) the reference of the related obliged entity, where applicable;
   - (g) where an advice or opinion refers to a natural person, the name, surname, date of birth, country of residence, nationality as well as the function (current or perspective) of the natural person in the obliged entity;
2. The information referred to in this Article shall be transmitted to the Authority for the first time by 15 July 2027, concerning information on advice or opinions provided to other authorities after the specified dates. In the case of the supervisory authorities in the non-financial sector and public authorities overseeing self-regulatory bodies, the transmission of information until 27 June 2028 will be done on a voluntary basis, as per Article 106(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620. The transmission shall take place no later than 30 working days delay following the provision of the opinion.

### <a id="article-5"></a>Article 5 — Information on supervisory activities

1. Supervisory authorities shall transmit to the central AML/CFT database the following information stipulated in Article 11(2), point (f) and in Article 11(2), point (g), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620:
   - (a) Reference of the obliged entity or obliged entities involved in the supervisory activity;
   - (b) the nature of the supervisory activity;
   - (c) the scope of the supervisory activity conducted;
   - (d) the start date of the supervisory activity;
   - (e) the end date of the supervisory activity;
   - (f) where applicable an indication if the supervisory activity is ongoing;
   - (g) the report of the thematic review produced by the supervisory authority at the conclusion of the thematic review. The report can be submitted in any of the official languages of the European Union;
   - (h) if other authorities participate in the supervisory activity, the details of these authorities and the nature of their involvement.
2. For the financial sector, the information referred to in this Article shall be transmitted to the Authority for the first time from 31 December 2027 concerning information on supervisory activities starting after the specified date. For the non-financial sector, the date shall be 31 December 2029.
3. For the financial sector, the information referred to in paragraph 1 points (a) to (d) and point (h) of this Article shall be submitted to the central AML/CFT database within 30 days of the start of the supervisory activity, with the exception of supervisory activities conducted off-site. The information referred to in paragraph 1 points (e) and where applicable, (g), as well as the information from points (a) to (d) and (h) for supervisory activities conducted off-site, shall be submitted no later than 30 working days after the end of the supervisory activity.
4. For the non-financial sector, the information referred to in paragraph 1 points (a) to (d) and point (h) of this Article shall be submitted to the central AML/CFT database within 30 working days of the start of the supervisory activity, with the exception of supervisory activities that are not part of a thematic review. The information referred to in paragraph 1 points (e) and where applicable, (g), as well as the information from points (a) to (d) and (h) for supervisory activities conducted either on-site of off-site that have resulted in the imposition of an administrative measure, pecuniary sanction or periodic penalty payment, shall be submitted no later than 30 working days after the end of the supervisory activity.
5. For the financial sector, by way of derogation from paragraphs 1 and 3, where a supervisory activity is conducted off-site, is not part of a thematic review or does not result in the imposition of an administrative measure, pecuniary sanction or periodic penalty payment, supervisory authorities shall only submit the information referred to in paragraph 1, points (a), (b), (c), (d) and (e). This information shall be submitted no later than 31 January of the calendar year following the one in which the supervisory activity was finalised.
6. For the non-financial sector, by way of derogation from paragraphs 1 and 4, where a supervisory activity is not part of a thematic review, or does not result in the imposition of an administrative measure, pecuniary sanction or periodic penalty payment, supervisory authorities shall only submit the information referred to in paragraph 1, point (b), aggregated by sector. This information shall be submitted no later than 31 January of the calendar year following the one in which the supervisory activity was finalised.
7. A supervisory activity shall be reported when it reflects the use of a supervisory tool as part of the supervisor's execution of its annual supervisory strategy or plan, or where such a tool is used on an ad hoc basis in response to specific events, emerging risks or concerns identified by the supervisory authority.

### <a id="article-6"></a>Article 6 — Statistical information on staffing and resources

1. For the purposes of Article 11(2), point (h), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620, supervisory authorities shall transmit to the central AML/CFT database statistical information on AML/CFT specialists allocated to AML/CFT tasks in full-time equivalents (FTEs) within the authority and, if applicable, within the self-regulatory body they oversee, as well as the annual budget (in Euros) available to the supervisory authority that is allocated to the development of activities and the acquisition and maintenance of resources dedicated to the prevention and fight against money laundering and terrorist financing.
2. Statistical information on AML/CFT specialists referred in Paragraph 1 shall be provided by distinguishing amongst the following categories, if available:
   - (a) policy and legal work;
   - (b) risk analysis;
   - (c) supervisory activities;
   - (d) enforcement activities;
3. The information referred to in this Article shall reflect the staffing and budgetary levels as of 31 December of the preceding year and shall be submitted no later than 31 March of the following calendar year. The first transmission shall take place by 31 March 2028 concerning statistical data of year 2027.

### <a id="article-7"></a>Article 7 — Additional information

1. The Authority may request additional information from supervisory authorities pursuant to Article 11(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620, where all of the following conditions are met:
   - (a) the requested information is necessary for the performance of one or more of the tasks listed in Article 5 Regulation (EU) 2024/1620;
   - (b) the Authority duly sets out:
     - the specific task listed in Article 5 Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 for which the additional information is required;
     - the justification of the necessity of the information; and
     - the intended use of the information.
2. Without prejudice to the conditions set out in the first paragraph, requests for additional information shall be proportionate to the objective pursued and the information shall be transmitted within 30 working days. With regards to the potential complexity of obtaining data, this deadline can be extended by the Authority. In case the supervisory authority cannot transmit the information to the Authority within the set deadline, it shall justify the reasons therefore and propose a new deadline to the Authority.
3. Supervisory authorities shall inform the Authority without undue delay if they do not have legal access to the information requested pursuant to Article 11(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620.

### <a id="article-8"></a>Article 8 — Prior Consent

1. Information submitted pursuant to Article 11, paragraph (2) and (3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 shall be treated as confidential.
2. Use of the information for the tasks of the Authority referred to in Article 5 Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 or within the supervisory system as defined in Article 2(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 does not require prior consent of the supervisory authority that originated the information. Prior consent shall be required before disclosure to third parties outside of the AML/CFT supervisory system.
3. By way of derogation from paragraph 2, confidential information may be shared without prior consent of the supervisory authority that provided the information where disclosure is authorised or required under Union law, including when the exchange of information is carried out with Financial Intelligence Units, supervisory authorities referred to in Article 2(44), points (c) and (d) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 or with authorities in charge of implementing targeted financial sanctions mentioned in Article 66 Directive (EU) 2024/1640. The same applies when the information received by the supervisor is used for the purposes set out in Article 67(3)(b) and (c) of the Directive (EU) 2024/1640.
4. Without prejudice to paragraph 2 and 3, prior consent should always be required whenever the information to be shared originated from a third country authority.
5. Where confidential information received from the supervisory authority that originated it is to be shared without prior consent pursuant to paragraph 3, the supervisory authority that originated the information shall where practicable, be notified prior to such transmission, and shall in any event be informed of the following without undue delay:
   - (a) The exact scope of the confidential information shared;
   - (b) The authority that received the confidential information;
   - (c) the legal basis of the obligation to disclose;
   - (d) the rationale(s) of the disclosure;
   - (e) the obligation in paragraph 2 does not apply to the disclosure of statistical, aggregated or anonymised data.

### <a id="article-9"></a>Article 9 — Format, procedures, technical specifications and data quality

1. The Authority shall set out and communicate to supervisory authorities the technical specifications for the submission and exchange of information referred to in this Regulation. Supervisory authorities shall conform to those specifications when submitting information through the central AML/CFT database.
2. The information shall be submitted in electronic form and, when appropriate, the submission process shall be automated.
3. Supervisory authorities shall declare to the Authority a person of appropriate seniority that will represent the supervisory authority when dealing with the Authority for the purpose of submitting information in accordance with this Regulation; they shall report to the Authority any change of that person(s) and they shall ensure that sufficient resources are dedicated for their reporting obligations under this Regulation to be discharged.
4. Supervisory authorities shall notify to the Authority a person or persons designated as tasked with the submission, the requests and the reception of information from the system established under this Regulation. Each designated contact point shall be granted access only to the data and system functionalities required for the performance of the specific tasks assigned to it under this Regulation.
5. Self-regulatory bodies shall make the declarations referred to in this paragraph to the public body overseeing those self-regulatory bodies and enabling their indirect submission.
6. Supervisory authorities shall ensure that the information transmitted to the Authority is complete, accurate, and up to date, and that it meets adequate standards of data quality to enable effective analysis and exchange through the central AML/CFT database.

### <a id="article-10"></a>Article 10 — Confidentiality

1. Without prejudice to provisions of this Regulation as to how information is analysed and made available to the supervisory authorities, information submitted to the Authority in accordance with this Regulation shall be subject to Articles 67 and 68 of [DIRECTIVE (EU) 2024/1640].
2. Members of the supervisory authorities' management bodies, and persons working for those authorities or who have worked for those authorities, even after their duties have ceased, shall be subject to professional secrecy requirements and shall not disclose information received in accordance with this Regulation, except in summary or aggregate form only such that natural and legal persons cannot be identified, without prejudice to cases where criminal law proceedings are pending.
3. Supervisory authorities receiving information in accordance with this Regulation shall treat information as confidential and shall use it only in the course of their supervisory activities with regard to the prevention and fight against money laundering and terrorist financing, including appeals against measures and sanctions taken and imposed by those authorities and in any court proceedings concerning supervisory activities.

### <a id="article-11"></a>Article 11 — Initial reporting following entry into force

1. Within [XXX] from the date of entry into force of this Regulation, supervisory authorities shall submit to the central AML/CFT database the information referred to in Articles 1 to 7 as applicable.
2. The initial submission shall reflect the status of the information as of the date of entry into force, unless otherwise provided.
