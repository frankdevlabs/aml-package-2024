# Adoption & legislative background

> Post-adoption tracker: this page records the package's **legislative history** (the equivalent of a
> "Commission proposal digest" for a file already adopted). The live workstream is the
> [AMLA pipeline](amla-pipeline.md), not the legislature.

## The package

The Commission proposed the AML package in **July 2021**. After the Ordinary Legislative Procedure, the three
core instruments were **adopted on 31 May 2024** and published in **OJ L, 19.6.2024**:

| Instrument | Proposal file | Adopted act | Digest |
|---|---|---|---|
| AMLR | 2021/0239(COD) | Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 | [digest](instruments/amlr-2024-1624.md) |
| AMLA | 2021/0240(COD) | Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 | [digest](instruments/amla-2024-1620.md) |
| AMLD6 | 2021/0250(COD) | Directive (EU) 2024/1640 | [digest](instruments/amld6-2024-1640.md) |

The wider package also includes the recast **[Transfer of Funds Regulation (EU) 2023/1113](instruments/tfr-2023-1113.md)**
(applicable since 30 December 2024), and **repeals [AMLD4 (Directive (EU) 2015/849)](instruments/repealed-amld4-2015-849.md)**
with effect from 10 July 2027.

## Objectives

A **single rulebook** (directly-applicable AMLR) to end national divergence; a **central authority** (AMLA)
for direct supervision of high-risk groups and to draft Level-2/3 standards; and a modernised institutional
layer (AMLD6) covering FIUs, supervisors and beneficial-ownership registers. Legal basis: **Article 114 TFEU**.

## Where to find the operative text

The package is adopted — authoritative consolidated texts are on EUR-Lex (see each [instrument digest](instruments/)).
The **moving** operative texts tracked under [`extracts/amla/`](../extracts/amla/) are the **draft Level-2/3
instruments** out for AMLA consultation. See [`amla-pipeline.md`](amla-pipeline.md).
