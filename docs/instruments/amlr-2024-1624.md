# Instrument: AMLR — Regulation (EU) 2024/1624

The **AML Regulation** — the directly-applicable "single rulebook" for obliged entities. The core of the package.

## What it does

Replaces most of the AMLD4 (Directive (EU) 2015/849) / national-law regime with a **single, directly-applicable
rulebook**: harmonised customer due diligence (CDD), beneficial-ownership rules, an expanded list of obliged
entities, the EU cash-payment limit, and the reporting framework. Because it is a Regulation, gatekeeper
obligations (CDD, reporting) flow directly from the AMLR rather than from national transposition.

## Provisions tracked here

- [CDD harmonisation](../provisions/cdd-harmonisation.md) — Art 28(1) RTS sets the detailed CDD information/verification fields.
- [UBO threshold "25% or more"](../provisions/ubo-threshold.md) — Art 52.
- [Cash limit](../provisions/cash-limit.md) — EU €10,000 cap (Art 80); member-state option to go lower.
- [Obliged-entity scope expansion](../provisions/scope-expansion-casps.md) — CASPs, crowdfunding, virtual IBANs, football, high-value goods.
- [Reporting](../provisions/reporting-suspicious.md) — FIU information-request deadlines.

## Level-2/3 mandates under the AMLR

Several first-wave AMLA instruments draw their legal basis from the AMLR — see [`amla-pipeline.md`](../amla-pipeline.md):
CDD RTS (Art 28(1)), business-relationship/occasional/linked-transaction triggers RTS (Art 19(9)), group-wide
requirements RTS (Arts 16(4)/17(3)), business-wide risk assessment guidelines (Art 10(4)), ongoing-monitoring
guidelines (Art 26(5)).
