# Repealed: AMLD4 — Directive (EU) 2015/849

The **fourth Anti-Money Laundering Directive** — the outgoing regime, transposed in the Netherlands as the
**Wet ter voorkoming van witwassen en financieren van terrorisme (Wwft)**.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **CELEX** | 32015L0849 |
| **ELI / EUR-Lex** | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/849/oj/eng |
| **Repealed with effect from** | **10 July 2027** (by [AMLD6, Directive (EU) 2024/1640](amld6-2024-1640.md)) |

## What replaces it

- The directly-applicable [**AMLR**](amlr-2024-1624.md) takes over the substantive obliged-entity rules
  (CDD, beneficial ownership, reporting, cash limit).
- [**AMLD6**](amld6-2024-1640.md) takes over the institutional/architecture layer (supervisors, FIUs, registers).

## Netherlands

The **Wwft** (which transposed AMLD4) is **replaced entirely by the Iwt as of 10 July 2027**. Statutory
references in internal AML documentation must move from Wwft/AMLD4 to the AMLR. See [`docs/netherlands.md`](../netherlands.md)
and the [single-rulebook provision](../provisions/cdd-harmonisation.md).

> **Scope note.** Do not confuse the repealed AMLD4 with the criminal-law Directive (EU) 2018/1673 ("6AMLD"),
> which is a separate instrument not tracked here.
