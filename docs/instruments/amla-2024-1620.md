# Instrument: AMLA Regulation — Regulation (EU) 2024/1620

Establishes the **Authority for Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AMLA)** — the
EU-level supervisor and single-rulebook drafter. The engine of the live implementation phase.

## Institutional status (as of 13 June 2026)

- **Seat:** Frankfurt am Main (MesseTurm, Friedrich-Ebert-Anlage 49, 60308). Operational since **1 July 2025**. — https://www.amla.europa.eu/governance_en
- **Chair:** **Bruna Szego** (ex-Banca d'Italia AML supervision unit), appointed by the Council 21 Jan 2025, joined AMLA 17 Feb 2025.
- **Executive Director:** **Nicolas Vasse** (ex-ESMA Head of Operations), appointed 1 Jul 2025 (announced 4 Jul 2025), took office 16 Sep 2025. — https://www.amla.europa.eu/resources/news-articles/amla-appoints-nicolas-vasse-executive-director_en
- **Governance:** Executive Board (Chair + five full-time members incl. a Vice-Chair) and a General Board in two compositions (supervisory and FIU). Selection of the fifth Executive Board member opened by the Commission 2 Jul 2025 (after the May 2025 withdrawal of Marcus Pleyer). Executive Board Member **Rikke-Louise Ørum Petersen** has opened AMLA's public hearings.
- **Milestones:** All AML/CFT mandates and functions transferred from the **EBA** to AMLA effective end-Dec 2025 / Jan 2026. Per AMLA's "About AMLA" timeline, staff ~**120 at end-2025**, reaching a "cruising capacity of about 430 by end-2027" (200+ on direct supervision). AMLA selects **40 of the most complex, high-risk financial institutions/groups** for direct supervision **during 2027**, with direct supervision starting **2028**.

## What it does

- **Direct supervision** of ~40 selected high-risk financial groups via Joint Supervisory Teams (from 2028); indirect oversight of national supervisors for the rest. — [provision](../provisions/amla-direct-supervision.md)
- **Single rulebook**: drafts RTS/ITS and issues guidelines (Level-2/3) — the [AMLA pipeline](../amla-pipeline.md).
- Coordinates and supports FIUs.

## Level-2/3 mandates under the AMLAR

The supervisory-cooperation ITS (Art 15(3) AMLAR) and the selection-of-entities RTS (Art 12(7) AMLAR) draw
their basis from this Regulation — see [`amla-pipeline.md`](../amla-pipeline.md). Pecuniary-sanctions RTS is
under AMLD6 Art 53(10), not the AMLAR.

## Amendments in force

**Regulation (EU) 2025/2088** (8 Oct 2025; OJ L, 2025/2088, 21.10.2025) — the omnibus "as regards certain
reporting requirements in the fields of financial services and investment support" — amends seven acts (the
ESRB Regulation, the three ESA Regulations, the SRM Regulation, InvestEU and the AMLA Regulation). Its
**Article 7** makes four changes to AMLAR, in force since **10 November 2025** (the 20th day after OJ
publication — the date reflected in the [10 Nov 2025 consolidation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1620/2025-11-10/eng)):

1. **Art 5(1), new point (k)** — AMLA is tasked to assist the Commission in reviewing the application of the
   RTS/ITS it drafts and the guidelines/recommendations it issues, and to propose amendments to remove
   redundant or obsolete reporting requirements, ensure proportionate and consistent reporting requirements,
   and address regulatory gaps.
2. **Art 55(1), new subparagraphs** — in its opinions AMLA may address the functioning of legislative acts in
   force, including the appropriateness of removing redundant or obsolete reporting requirements; the
   Commission may, where appropriate, follow up with a legislative proposal.
3. **Art 88, new paragraph 3a** — a professional-secrecy carve-out so the confidentiality duty does not
   prevent AMLA from exchanging information with "the other authorities" as defined in the new Art 92a(12).
4. **New Art 92a — "Exchange of information between authorities and with other entities"** — a horizontal
   information-sharing framework: AMLA shares information it holds with other authorities on request (and
   requests from them, rather than directly from obliged entities, where possible), subject to notification
   duties, professional-secrecy and data-protection safeguards (incl. Regs (EU) 2016/679 and 2018/1725), and
   optional memoranda of understanding.

These are technical / reporting-burden changes; they do not alter AMLA's supervisory architecture or the
Level-2/3 mandates tracked in [`amla-pipeline.md`](../amla-pipeline.md).

## Notes

- DNB represents the Dutch Wwft supervisors on the AMLA General Board (supervisory composition).
- Legacy EBA AML/CFT guidelines remain valid until replaced by AMLA instruments (Art 54(5) AMLAR).
- The EUR-Lex CELEX `32024R1620` XML notice is periodically refreshed (e.g. **1 July 2026**,
  **13 April 2026**) with RDF metadata only — a notice-generation refresh (language manifestations /
  descriptors / cross-references), distinct from a substantive change. The **10 November 2025**
  consolidation, by contrast, **is** substantive: it incorporates the Reg (EU) 2025/2088 amendments (see
  [Amendments in force](#amendments-in-force)). Beyond that amending act and the Spanish-only 5 Feb 2025
  corrigendum, no further amendment or consolidation has issued.
