# Instrument: AMLA Regulation — Regulation (EU) 2024/1620

Establishes the **Authority for Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AMLA)** — the
EU-level supervisor and single-rulebook drafter. The engine of the live implementation phase.

## Institutional status (as of 13 June 2026)

- **Seat:** Frankfurt am Main (MesseTurm, Friedrich-Ebert-Anlage 49, 60308). Operational since **1 July 2025**. — https://www.amla.europa.eu/governance_en
- **Chair:** **Bruna Szego** (ex-Banca d'Italia AML supervision unit), appointed by the Council 21 Jan 2025, joined AMLA 17 Feb 2025.
- **Executive Director:** **Nicolas Vasse** (ex-ESMA Head of Operations), appointed 1 Jul 2025 (announced 4 Jul 2025), took office 16 Sep 2025. — https://www.amla.europa.eu/resources/news-articles/amla-appoints-nicolas-vasse-executive-director_en
- **Governance:** Executive Board (Chair + five full-time members incl. a Vice-Chair) and a General Board in two compositions (supervisory and FIU). Selection of the fifth Executive Board member opened by the Commission 2 Jul 2025 (after the May 2025 withdrawal of Marcus Pleyer). Executive Board Member **Rikke-Louise Ørum Petersen** has opened AMLA's public hearings.
- **Milestones:** All AML/CFT mandates and functions transferred from the **EBA** to AMLA effective end-Dec 2025 / Jan 2026. Per AMLA's "About AMLA" timeline, staff ~**120 at end-2025**, reaching a "cruising capacity of about 430 by end-2027" (200+ on direct supervision). AMLA selects **40 of the most complex, high-risk financial institutions/groups** for direct supervision **during 2027**, with direct supervision starting **2028**.

## What it does

- **Direct supervision** of ~40 selected high-risk financial groups via Joint Supervisory Teams (from 2028); indirect oversight of national supervisors for the rest. — [provision](../provisions/amla-direct-supervision.md)
- **Single rulebook**: drafts RTS/ITS and issues guidelines (Level-2/3) — the [AMLA pipeline](../amla-pipeline.md).
- Coordinates and supports FIUs.

## Level-2/3 mandates under the AMLAR

The supervisory-cooperation ITS (Art 15(3) AMLAR) and the selection-of-entities RTS (Art 12(7) AMLAR) draw
their basis from this Regulation — see [`amla-pipeline.md`](../amla-pipeline.md). Pecuniary-sanctions RTS is
under AMLD6 Art 53(10), not the AMLAR.
