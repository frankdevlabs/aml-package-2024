# Instrument: AMLD6 — Directive (EU) 2024/1640

The **sixth Anti-Money Laundering Directive** — the institutional/architecture layer Member States must
transpose (supervisors, FIUs, beneficial-ownership registers, access). The part the Dutch **Iwt** transposes.

> **Naming caution.** "AMLD6"/"6MLD" here = **Directive (EU) 2024/1640**. Do **not** confuse it with the
> criminal-law Directive (EU) 2018/1673 (also informally "6AMLD") — see [`tracker.yaml`](../../tracker.yaml)
> `keep_apart`.

## What it does

- Re-regulates the **supervisory architecture** (Member-State competent authorities) and **FIUs** (broader
  powers, incl. temporary transaction suspension).
- **Beneficial-ownership registers** and discrepancy-reporting (14-day duty under the AMLR); strengthens the
  role of company registers (in NL, the KvK UBO-register). — [UBO threshold](../provisions/ubo-threshold.md)
- Cross-border cooperation and access to information.

## Level-2/3 mandate under AMLD6

The **pecuniary sanctions, administrative measures & periodic penalty payments RTS (Art 53(10) AMLD6)** is in
AMLA's first wave — see [`amla-pipeline.md`](../amla-pipeline.md). The RTS on the inherent/residual risk
profile of obliged entities (Art 40(2) AMLD6) was finalised end-2025 and applies from 31 December 2027.

## National transposition

- **Netherlands:** the **Implementatiewet ter voorkoming van witwassen en terrorismefinanciering (Iwt)**
  transposes AMLD6 and replaces the Wwft entirely from 10 July 2027. Status and detail: [`docs/netherlands.md`](../netherlands.md).
