# Instrument: Transfer of Funds Regulation (recast) — Regulation (EU) 2023/1113

The recast **Transfer of Funds Regulation (TFR)** — part of the wider AML package, extending "travel rule"
information requirements to **crypto-asset transfers**.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Short** | TFR (recast) |
| **CELEX** | 32023R1113 |
| **ELI / EUR-Lex** | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1113/oj/eng |
| **Applicable since** | **30 December 2024** |

## Why it is here

The TFR is the part of the package that applied **first** (30 Dec 2024), ahead of the AMLR/AMLD6 application
date of 10 July 2027. It requires originator/beneficiary information to travel with crypto-asset transfers
(the "travel rule"), dovetailing with the AMLR's bringing of all crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) into
the obliged-entity scope — see [scope expansion](../provisions/scope-expansion-casps.md).

This repo tracks the TFR only as **context**; the live workstream is the AMLR/AMLA/AMLD6 implementation.
