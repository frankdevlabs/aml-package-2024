# Netherlands — national implementation

The Dutch leg of the package: transposition of [AMLD6](instruments/amld6-2024-1640.md) via the **Iwt**, the
national cash limit, the UBO threshold change, and the contested "lastenluw" framing. (The directly-applicable
[AMLR](instruments/amlr-2024-1624.md) needs no transposition — gatekeeper obligations flow from it directly.)

## a. Implementatiewet ter voorkoming van witwassen en terrorismefinanciering (Iwt)

- **What it does:** transposes AMLD6; **replaces the Wwft entirely as of 10 July 2027**; re-regulates the
  supervisory architecture (DNB, AFM, BFT and others) and FIU-Nederland (broader powers, incl. temporary
  transaction suspension). Because the AMLR is directly applicable, CDD/reporting obligations flow from the
  AMLR, not the Iwt.
- **Consultation:** internet consultation ran **4 July – 29 August 2025**
  (https://www.internetconsultatie.nl/implementatiewettervoorkomingvanwitwassenenterrorismefinanciering/b1);
  consultation report published. Law-firm analysis: [Finnius](https://finnius.com/en/consultation-on-implementation-act-on-anti-money-laundering-and-anti-terrorist-financing/).
- **Current status (13 June 2026): NOT yet submitted (ingediend) to the Tweede Kamer.** The Council of
  Ministers approved on **24 April 2026** sending the bill to the **Afdeling advisering van de Raad van State**
  for advice — *"De minister van Financiën zal het voorstel om advies aanhangig doen maken bij de Afdeling
  advisering van de Raad van State"* ([Besluitenlijst ministerraad 24 april 2026](https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/16cf634e-a9e2-4411-b302-b1264198c3a5/)).
  **No Kamerstuk dossier number (36XXX) exists yet** — one is assigned only on submission. Current pipeline
  marker: **"at the Raad van State for advice."**
- **Lead ministry:** Financiën, with Justitie en Veiligheid and Economische Zaken co-responsible. Only a
  *concept* memorie van toelichting exists (the consultation version); the definitive MvT appears with the
  Kamerstuk on submission. The Iwt will be elaborated further in a separate **implementatiebesluit** (to be
  consulted later).
- **Wetgevingskalender:** https://wetgevingskalender.overheid.nl/Regeling/WGK027204 *(blocks automated
  fetching — verify manually)*.
- Advisory inputs include the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (toets, Dec 2025) and the Openbaar Ministerie
  (advice, 4 Sep 2025).

## b. Wet plan van aanpak witwassen (cash limit) — dossier 36.228

- The **€3,000 cash-payment ban for goods** entered into force **1 January 2026** (Staatsblad 2025, 362;
  inwerkingtredings-KB of 3 Nov 2025, published 18 Nov 2025). Confirmed by
  [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/aangifte_betalen_en_toezicht/wwft-voorkomen-van-witwassen-en-terrorismefinanciering/verplichtingen/verbod-cash-3000)
  and [AMLC](https://www.amlc.nl/de-cashlimiet-de-verboden-contante-betalingen/).
- Applies to professional/commercial dealers in goods, pawnshops and art dealers; €3,000 limit anchored in
  Art 1f Wwft. **Article IIA** (a cash-*acceptance* duty under €3,000) is **not yet in force** pending an
  exemptions decree (in consultation). The Eerste Kamer adopted the bill **10 June 2025**. The limit
  **extends to services from 10 July 2027** under the AMLR (NL using the Art 80 AMLR member-state option to go
  below the EU €10,000 default).
- Dossiers: [Eerste Kamer 36228](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36228_wet_plan_van_aanpak) ·
  [Tweede Kamer 36228](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/wetsvoorstellen/detail?cfg=wetsvoorsteldetails&qry=wetsvoorstel:36228).
- See the [cash-limit provision](provisions/cash-limit.md).

## c. UBO threshold change ("≥25%")

AMLR lowers the beneficial-ownership threshold from "**more than 25%**" to "**25% or more**" (Art 52 AMLR,
direct and indirect interests; the Commission may set a lower threshold of 15% — or lower — for high-risk
sectors). Confirmed for NL by [Loyens & Loeff](https://www.loyensloeff.com/insights/news--events/news/new-eu-legislation-for-the-identification-and-registration-of-ubos/).
Interacts with the KvK UBO-register; AMLD6 strengthens the KvK role and adds a discrepancy-reporting duty
(14 days under the AMLR). See the [UBO provision](provisions/ubo-threshold.md).

## d. "Lastenluw" / risk-based approach — contested

- The government frames implementation as "**lastenluw**" (minimal administrative burden) and risk-based; the
  concept MvT and the cabinet's "Overzicht lastenluwe implementatiekeuzes AML-pakket" (14 May 2025, document
  2025D2115) set this out: https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/497b3bd5-6966-41e3-aa7a-7fb1e8a4870a/file
- **However, this framing is contested.** The Adviescollege toetsing regeldruk (ATR), in advice **ATR4058
  (29 September 2025)** titled "Implementatie Europees antiwitwaspakket niet lastenluw", found: *"Het kabinet
  geeft aan dat Nederland de Europese regels lastenluw wil implementeren. ATR constateert dat dit op een
  aantal onderdelen niet gebeurt. Dat maakt de Nederlandse regelgeving meer belastend dan vanuit Europa
  noodzakelijk is."* Record "lastenluw" as the government's stated **intent**, not an uncontested outcome.
  See [`fault-lines.md`](fault-lines.md).

## e. Other Dutch developments (to June 2026)

- **DNB:** from 2027, DNB will calculate institutions' risk scores using the AMLA methodology in the RTS under
  Art 40(2) AMLD6; institutions should adapt data registration and register cross-border activities from 2026
  ([DNB IRAP/AMLA notice](https://www.dnb.nl/nieuws-voor-de-sector/toezicht-2025/q4/wijzigingen-in-irap-door-komst-amla-bereid-u-voor-op-2026-en-2027/)).
  DNB's [European AML/CFT framework hub](https://www.dnb.nl/voor-de-sector/open-boek-toezicht/wet-regelgeving/europees-amlcft-kader/).
- DNB represents the Dutch Wwft supervisors on the AMLA General Board (supervisory composition). DNB notes
  AMLA's risk-based approach may diverge from existing DNB Q&As/Good Practices Wwft and the NVB Risk-Based
  Industry Baselines ([Deloitte analysis](https://www.deloitte.com/nl/nl/services/consulting-financial/perspectives/amla-and-amlr-a-blessing-or-curse-for-dutch-financial-institutions.html)).
- The NVB filed a position paper on UBO/CDD: https://www.nvb.nl/media/5168/nvb-aml-ubo-position-paper.pdf
