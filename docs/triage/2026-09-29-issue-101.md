## Assessment
**Substantive and in-scope.** The authentic PDF (~91 KB) is Aanhangsel Handelingen II **2026/27, nr. 117**
(landing-page metadata `aanhangselNummer=117`, `vergaderjaar=2026-2027`; the "AH 117" header on the PDF),
kenmerk **2026Z13645**, answered by min. **Van Weel** (Justitie en Veiligheid) mede namens Financiën, **received
25 September 2026**. The subject (crypto payments for online CSAM) is not itself the AML package, but the answers
engage the **tracked file** by name — Dir (EU) **2024/1640** (AMLD6) and Reg (EU) **2024/1624** (AMLR) as the
"AML-pakket" replacing AMLD5 in Jul 2027 — **not** the criminal-law 2018/1673 sibling. Four dated confirmations:

1. **Implementation ongoing (A6):** *"AMLD5 wordt in juli 2027 vervangen door de zesde antiwitwasrichtlijn
   (AMLD6) en de antiwitwasverordening (AMLR) ... Aan de implementatie van de AMLD6 en AMLR wordt op dit moment
   gewerkt."*
2. **AMLR information-sharing legal basis (A10, A12):** there is currently no wettelijke grondslag for
   information-sharing between gatekeepers (incl. CASPs); *"de nieuwe Europese anti-witwasverordening die per
   10 juli 2027 in werking treedt, creëert nieuwe mogelijkheden tot het delen van gegevens"* — the AMLR/Art 75
   territory the repo already tracks via the AMLA/EDPB information-sharing-partnerships Guidelines.
3. **AMLA crypto-priority role (A7–A9):** AMLA coordinates FIU **Joint Analysis Teams (JAT's)**, uniformises FIU
   information exchange, flags crypto/CASPs as a prominent priority; NL is closely involved via FIU-Nederland and
   the AML supervisors and sits in the **C7 coalition** (BE, DE, FR, IT, ES, SE).
4. **CASP supervision context (A5, A6):** under MiCAR passporting CASPs report unusual transactions only to the
   FIU of the licensing MS (recovered via FIU-to-FIU exchange); the **TFR travel rule** applies with the **AFM**
   supervising risk-based measures against non-compliant foreign providers.

Confirms the package applies from **10 July 2027** (already recorded). Unlike issue #94 (Aanhangsel nr. 2923),
this answer carries **no new transposition-*option* choice** — its value is a dated ministerial statement on the
package's crypto/CASP dimension. Integration is therefore proportionate; **no STATUS.md milestone or
institution-stance shift** (no new procedural step).
