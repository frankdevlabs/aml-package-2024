  enforcing AML/CFT breaches: a **four-step methodology** (shared gravity indicators → classification into **four
  gravity levels** → common criteria for selecting the enforcement outcome → applies across all financial and
  non-financial sectors), so "the same breach in the same circumstances leads to the same enforcement outcome".

  53(10)", 9 July 2026** (PDF retrieved and committed; sha256 25aad176…f44fcb0). This is the respondent-feedback

  "COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION" operative annex.

This is the status-changing event for **item 4** of the AMLA pipeline: the **draft RTS on pecuniary sanctions,
administrative measures & periodic penalty payments (Art 53(10) AMLD6)** the repo already tracks at
[`../../extracts/amla/RTS-pecuniary-sanctions-art53-10_consultation-paper.md`](../../extracts/amla/RTS-pecuniary-sanctions-art53-10_consultation-paper.md).

## Assessment

**Substantive and in scope.** Article 53(10) AMLD6 = **Directive (EU) 2024/1640** — the tracked package, not the
`keep_apart` sibling Directive (EU) 2018/1673. The flag comes in on a Tier-2 media feed, but per the skill's
"low-tier source can carry Tier-1-grade substance" rule it is upgraded to the **amla-instrument** playbook because it
reports a primary AMLA milestone (consultation closed → final RTS to Commission).

**Operative text not yet retrievable.** What AMLA has published so far is the press release and the consultation
**feedback**. The **final RTS operative text** (the Commission Delegated Regulation supplementing AMLD6) is being
submitted to the Commission and is **not** published as a transcribable instrument yet — so there is **no
`_final.md` sibling** to build. This is registered **metadata + feedback only**, with `pending_operative_text: true`
so the work re-surfaces when the final RTS is published (as a Commission Delegated Regulation in the OJ, per the
pipeline's benchmark). The consulted draft extract stays the diff baseline for that eventual `_final.md`.
