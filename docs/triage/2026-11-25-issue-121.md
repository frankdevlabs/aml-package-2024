## Assessment

**Substantive and in-scope.** The legal basis is **AMLD6 = Directive (EU) 2024/1640** — the tracked file, not
the `keep_apart` criminal-law Directive (EU) 2018/1673. This is a brand-new primary Level-3 instrument (a
diffable draft operative text), so per the skill's upgrade rule it is handled with the **amla-instrument**
workflow (new `extracts/amla/` slice + registrations + pipeline/positions rows), not left as a bare procedural
note.

It is a **distinct instrument** from the already-tracked Art 53(10) AMLD6 **RTS** (item 4), and sits downstream
of it: the RTS fixes the gravity indicators and the four gravity categories; these Guidelines take the category
as given and set, per category and legal form, a **base amount expressed as a % of a relevant financial
metric** (total annual turnover for legal persons / total gross annual income for natural persons) through a
four-step method. They stop at the base amount — the Art 53(6) AMLD6 aggravating/mitigating adjustments to the
final sanction are out of scope. The draft is a **clean** PDF, so plain `pdftotext` suffices (no `pymupdf`).

Operative content (Table 1): legal persons **0.25–0.50%** (Cat 1–2) / **0.50–5.00%** (Cat 3–4); natural persons
**0.50–1.00%** / **1.00–10.00%**. Applies from **10 Jul 2027** (10 Jul 2029 for Art 3(3)(n)/(o) entities);
does not apply to sanction proceedings initiated before 10 Jul 2027.
