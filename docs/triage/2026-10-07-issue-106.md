## Assessment

**Substantive and in-scope.** This is a brand-new Level-2 draft RTS under **AMLAR** — a primary instrument of exactly the kind this repo's live spine tracks, not procedural noise and not a `keep_apart` sibling (it is AMLAR, Reg (EU) 2024/1620, not the 2018/1673 criminal-law "6AMLD"). Per the skill's tier/type rule, the Tier-1 hub signal is run through the **AMLA-instrument core workflow**: register + transcribe the operative draft, then cascade into the pipeline/positions/status/timeline layers.

Substance of the draft RTS (operative section 4 of the consultation paper): 11 operative articles + Annex I (categories of obliged entities) + Annex II (data points per article). It specifies the supervisory information supervisors transmit to AMLA's central AML/CFT database and the procedures/formats/timelines. Three streams: (1) data on **supervisors themselves** (powers, tasks, staffing/budget — Arts 1, 6); (2) data on **obliged entities** (inherent/residual risk profile and the underlying data points, drawn from the **Art 40(2) AMLD6** RTS — Art 2, with the draft stressing **no new reporting burden on obliged entities**); (3) data on **sanctions / administrative measures** (Art 3), **advice/opinions** raising ML/TF concerns (Art 4), **supervisory activities** (Art 5), plus additional-information requests (Art 7), prior consent (Art 8), format/data-quality (Art 9) and confidentiality (Art 10). Phased start: first financial-sector submissions from **15 Jul 2027**; non-financial-sector transmission **voluntary until 27 Jun 2028** (Art 106(1) AMLAR); deferred dates for football clubs/agents (AMLR applies to them from 10 Jul 2029).

Source artefacts preserved verbatim per the transcription guide: the final entry-into-force clause is **mislabelled "Article 3"** (it follows Article 11), and a stray page-header line referencing the Art 53(10) final report appears before Annex I (omitted as a non-operative artefact; both documented in the extract header).
