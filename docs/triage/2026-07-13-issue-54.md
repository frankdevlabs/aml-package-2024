## Assessment

**Substantive and in scope.** Legal basis is **Directive (EU) 2024/1640** — this repo's AMLD6, not the
`keep_apart` criminal-law 6AMLD (2018/1673). It is a genuine new Level-2 instrument in AMLA's live pipeline, so
it warrants a full `extracts/amla/` slice and register/pipeline/positions rows (the CLAUDE.md core workflow), not
a mere STATUS note.

**Scope subtlety (keep apart within the pipeline):** Art 40(2) AMLD6 is delivered by **two** RTS. A
**financial-sector** RTS on the inherent/residual risk profile was already published as a draft in **Dec 2025**
(Final Report 16 Dec 2025) and is recorded in the pipeline's "Verified events". This new consultation is the
**non-financial-sector** companion — same legal basis, distinct instrument. Both the extract and the pipeline
note make the distinction explicit.

**One-issue discipline:** the 13 Jul hub re-check also showed two *other* newly-opened July consultations (FIU
cross-border-exchange RTS, 6 Jul; suspicions/records reporting ITS, 2 Jul). Those are **out of scope for #54**
(tracked under their own issues, cf. #40) and were deliberately **not** integrated here — only flagged in the
pipeline verification note.
