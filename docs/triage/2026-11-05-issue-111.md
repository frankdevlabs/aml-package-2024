## Assessment

**In-scope and substantive.** The answer concerns the Dutch UBO register — the national-implementation leg of
**AMLR (Reg (EU) 2024/1624) Art 52** and **AMLD6 (Dir (EU) 2024/1640)**, the file this repo tracks. Not the
`keep_apart` criminal-law Directive (EU) 2018/1673. The full text (extracted from the published PDF) confirms
several new facts not yet recorded in the repo:

1. **New UBO-register enforcer: DFEI.** As of **1 January 2026** the Bureau Economische Handhaving (BEH) and
   the Bureau Toezicht Wwft (BTWwft) merged into the **Dienst Financieel-Economische Integriteit (DFEI)**, part
   of the Ministerie van Financiën. The minister designated DFEI (per 1 Jan 2026) as the authority enforcing
   the UBO-register registration obligations. (The repo's `netherlands.md` describes the DNB/AFM/BFT
   supervisory architecture but did not name the UBO-register enforcement body.)
2. **Enforcement stance.** DFEI sends a *final-warning* letter with a deadline before any fine/sanction;
   approach is risk-based and proportionate; goodwilling entities that register (or ask for help) after the
   letter face no sanction. The minister **declines** to pause enforcement/fines (Vraag 5), stating the
   obligation flows directly from the European anti-money-laundering directive, which itself requires
   enforcement.
3. **Reaffirmation of "10 July 2027" + "lastenluw" (Vraag 8).** *"Op 10 juli 2027 treden de zesde
   antiwitwasrichtlijn en de antiwitwasverordening in werking. Hierin staan gedetailleerdere regels op het
   gebied van UBO's. Ik zorg ervoor dat deze nieuwe Europese regels lastenluw worden geïmplementeerd."* — ties
   to the contested "lastenluw" framing in `netherlands.md §d`.
4. **UBO-determination handleiding in preparation.** The minister, with DFEI, the KvK and the minister of JenV,
   is preparing a guide on determining the UBO for common structures such as stichtingen and verenigingen, with
   sector input — to make registration easier and give a uniform reading of the UBO concept.
5. Context (not new): the UBO register took effect **27 September 2020**; pre-existing entities' 18-month
   deadline expired **27 March 2022**; the obligation covers BVs, stichtingen and verenigingen.

This is a ministerial *position/clarification* on implementation, not a procedural step in the Iwt pipeline.
No change to the Iwt status (still at the Raad van State) and no change to any tracked provision feature
(checked against `STATUS.md` "What changed" / `docs/what-changed.md`: the UBO-threshold row is unaffected).
