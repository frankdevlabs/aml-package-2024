## What was flagged
New top item in the package-filtered Kamervragen feed: **Aanhangsel nr. 748** (vraagnummer **2026Z19353**,
vergaderjaar 2026-2027), minister **Herbert (Economische Zaken en Klimaat)**, ontvangen/gepubliceerd

DC.title is truncated at "over regels …"; the feed matched the in-scope keyword filter.

Confirmed by fetching the PDF (`ah-1281744.pdf`, 81 pp.): the document is **not** an AML-specific Q&A. It is
the government's point-by-point answer to a **VVD "regeldruk" (red-tape-reduction) inventory of 97 rules**
across every ministry (RI&E, ontslagtoets, horeca-vergunningen, bouw, etc.). It is the companion answer to the
earlier Aanhangsel nr. 344 in the same series.

**Three of the 97 items are in-scope for this tracker (the AML Package):**

- **Item 3 — "Aanpassing Wwft" (FIN & JenV).** Q: adapt the Wwft so the reporting duty aligns with the EU
  **"verdachte"** (suspicious) norm instead of the Dutch **"ongebruikelijke"** (unusual) norm. A (verbatim):
  *"Ja, dit vloeit reeds voort uit de rechtstreekse werking van de nieuwe Europese regels. De regels van de
  antiwitwasverordening voor het melden van transacties komen namelijk in de plaats van de regeling op grond
  van de Wwft. De Europese verordening verplicht dat poortwachters tegen witwassen per 10 juli 2027 verdachte
  transacties melden bij de FIU Nederland."* The Wwft unusual-transaction regime (and the FIE indicators)
  **"komt dus te vervallen."**
- **Item 88 — "Materialiteitsdrempel in de terugmeldplicht UBO" (FIN).** Q: introduce a **materiality
  threshold** and **bulk reporting (verzamelmeldingen)** for discrepancy reports between the UBO-register and
  CDD. A: the cabinet **"zal de suggestie … onderzoeken"** — ongoing talks with gatekeepers (mainly the large
  banks, which file most terugmeldingen) aim to ensure reports are made only on a **material** discrepancy;
  reporting minor typos "is vaak niet materieel."
- **Item 89 — "Schrap de dubbele opgave van bestuurders als pseudo-UBO" (FIN).** Q: drop separate pseudo-UBO
  filing and auto-derive it from the Handelsregister. A: **not fully possible under EU law**; the cabinet
  shares the simplification aim; KVK is building a **pre-filled** UBO form. From **10 July 2027** UBO
  registration flows **directly from the antiwitwasverordening (AMLR)**, which requires **more personal data**
  than the Handelsregister holds for directors, so a one-to-one carry-over is not possible.

No reference to the criminal-law Directive 2018/1673 (the `keep_apart` sibling).

## Assessment
**Substantive and in-scope.** The three items are a dated, on-the-record **government position** on AMLR direct
effect vs. the national Wwft, on the UBO discrepancy-reporting duty, and on UBO registration mechanics — and
they sit squarely in the repo's "lastenluw"/regeldruk fault line (VVD pressing for burden reduction; the
cabinet answering within the bounds of directly-applicable EU law). Items 3 and 89 mainly **reaffirm** facts
the repo already records (AMLR replaces the Wwft reporting regime at 10 Jul 2027; UBO under AMLR) but now with a
concrete parliamentary citation; **item 88 is genuinely new** — a cabinet commitment to *investigate* a
materiality threshold + bulk reporting for UBO terugmeldingen.

Scope rule applied: concerns **Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 (AMLR)** / NL transposition (`file_id`), not the
criminal-law `keep_apart` directive. In-scope.
