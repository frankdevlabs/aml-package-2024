## Assessment

**Substantive and in-scope.** Confirmed by fetching the landing page and the attached PDF
(*"ADVISORY NOTE — ML/TF risks resulting from the end of the MiCAR Transitional Period and possible
mitigating measures"*, 3 pp., 29 Jun 2026, committed at
`sources/amla/AMLA-ADVISORY-MICAR_advisory-note_2026-06-29.pdf`).

- **In-scope:** the MiCAR transitional period (Art 143(3) Reg (EU) 2023/1114 grandfathering) ends
  **1 July 2026**; unauthorised VASPs must exit and customers move to authorised CASPs, who are AMLR
  obliged entities. This is AMLA soft guidance on the AML Package, not a `keep_apart` sibling (it is
  not Dir (EU) 2018/1673) and not an AI-Act/other-file item.
- **Nature:** a **stand-alone advisory note**, *not* a Level-2/3 RTS/ITS/Guidelines instrument and
  not part of the formal mandate pipeline — so it gets a verified-events bullet, not a row in the
  consultation status table. It is non-binding ("possible mitigating measures").
- **Substance (per the operative table):** risks + mitigations for four actors —
  - *Unauthorised VASPs:* weakened controls / illicit-flow concealment during exit → structured,
    documented wind-down with maintained AML/CFT governance, enhanced monitoring, up-to-date CDD and
    suspicious-activity reporting until activities cease.
  - *Authorised CASPs:* sudden risk-profile shifts / transaction-monitoring strain from inflows →
    scalable monitoring and staffing, effective CDD and risk integration; **VASP customers must not
    be subject to blanket de-risking** — assess individually under a risk-based approach with EDD
    where higher risk is identified.
  - *AML/CFT supervisors:* blind spots / inconsistent standards → risk-based oversight of wind-down
    and customer transfers, cross-jurisdiction coordination and timely information sharing to avoid
    regulatory arbitrage.
  - *FIUs:* new typologies from mass transfers → enhanced cross-border and domestic cooperation and
    timely intelligence dissemination.
- The "What changed" table already records CASPs entering scope
  ([`docs/provisions/scope-expansion-casps.md`](../provisions/scope-expansion-casps.md)); this note
  adds no new package feature, only AMLA's transition-risk guidance.
