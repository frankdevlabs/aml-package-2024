## What was flagged

The CELEX `32024R1620` object notice now references a **consolidated version** `02024R1620-20251110`
(10 Nov 2025) alongside the original `02024R1620-20240619`. The tracker flags this as implying an amending
act / corrigendum took effect, and asks triage to confirm on EUR-Lex what the 10 Nov 2025 consolidation
reflects and whether it touches any tracked AMLA provision. It notes this **supersedes #29** (1 Jul 2026),
which found "metadata only, no consolidation".

## Assessment

**Substantive — a genuine amending act, previously mis-attributed.** Confirmed by fetching the consolidated
text (browser tier, WAF workaround) and the amending act itself:

- The 10 Nov 2025 consolidation's "Amended by" table lists **Regulation (EU) 2025/2088 of 8 October 2025**
  (OJ L, 2025/2088, 21.10.2025; CELEX 32025R2088) as amendment **M1** — the financial-services
  "reporting-requirements simplification" omnibus (amends the ESRB Reg, the three ESA Regs, the SRM Reg,
  InvestEU **and** AMLAR).
- Its **Article 7** amends AMLAR (Reg (EU) 2024/1620), in force since **10 November 2025** (20th day after
  the 21 Oct 2025 OJ publication — which is exactly the consolidation date):
  1. **Art 5(1), new point (k)** — AMLA to assist the Commission in reviewing application of the RTS/ITS it
     drafts and its guidelines/recommendations, and propose amendments (remove redundant/obsolete reporting
     requirements; ensure proportionate & consistent reporting; address regulatory gaps).
  2. **Art 55(1), new subparagraphs** — AMLA opinions may address the functioning of legislative acts in
     force (incl. removing redundant/obsolete reporting requirements); Commission may follow up with a
     legislative proposal.
  3. **Art 88, new para 3a** — professional-secrecy carve-out enabling info exchange with "the other
     authorities" as defined in the new Art 92a(12).
  4. **New Art 92a** — "Exchange of information between authorities and with other entities": a horizontal
     info-sharing framework (share on request; request from authorities rather than obliged entities where
     possible; notification duties; GDPR/EUDPR safeguards; optional MoUs).

- **In scope:** CELEX 32024R1620 is AMLA, a tracked instrument (`file_id`). Not a `keep_apart` sibling.
- The repo currently **mis-records** this: the digest attributes the consolidation to nothing / implies the
  10 Nov 2025 consolidation is a metadata-only refresh, and its Notes bullet frames the only substantive
  correction as the Spanish-only 5 Feb 2025 corrigendum. That is wrong — the driver is Reg (EU) 2025/2088.
  Issue #29's "metadata only" conclusion was correct *for its own notice-refresh delta* but missed that the
  10 Nov 2025 consolidation it recorded already reflected this amending act.
- Substance is **technical** (reporting-burden simplification + horizontal info-sharing); it does not alter
  AMLA's supervisory architecture or the Level-2/3 mandates. So it corrects the instrument digest and
  chronology but is **not** a headline change to the AMLD4→package "What changed" story in `STATUS.md`.
