Three findings drive edits:

1. **`T2-04` is not a transient block.** The Wordfence page gives Block Reason *"Accessed a banned URL"* and
   states: *"if you operate a bot, crawler, AI system, or data aggregation service and wish to access or
   reuse our content, you must obtain a commercial licence."* That is a deliberate publisher policy against
   automated polling, not a rate-limit — waiting for the 2026-08-04 auto-pause is pointless, and re-adding
   the feed later would be re-entering a licensing dispute. Replace it, and record *why* so it is not
   restored. Its role in the watchlist is file-agnostic ("mirrors EU institutional outcomes invisible to
   direct polling"). Probed replacements: `finance.ec.europa.eu/rss_en` → 404;
   `finance.ec.europa.eu/news_en?…&format=rss` → 403; consilium press RSS → 403;
   **`ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/rss` → 200 `application/rss+xml`**, and it honours
   `pagesize=50` (35 KB, 50 items — a deep enough window that a weekday-cadence poll cannot skip an item).
   That is the replacement.

2. **`T1-04` is not flapping — the *client* is.** The resolver answers `303` and the representation depends
   on whether the `Accept` header survives the redirect: with `-L` + `Accept: application/xml;notice=object`
   three consecutive fetches were byte-identical (561,243 B, `content_type` `application/xml;notice=object`);
   with **no** `Accept` header the same URL returns 755,517 B of `application/rdf+xml` — which is the digest's
   "766 KB FMX act body". The cron prompt already prescribes `-L` + `Accept` (`prompt-aml.md` line 93), so
   prescription is not the gap: there is **no post-fetch guard**, so an off-spec response gets hashed as if it
   were content. Fix = assert `content_type` contains `notice=object` and the payload is ≈561 KB before
   hashing; otherwise retry, then log unreachable rather than record a hash.

3. **The digest's guess about #58 is wrong — do not act on it.** `docs/triage/2026-07-17-issue-58.md` did not
   rest on a hash delta: it confirmed against EUR-Lex that the 10 Nov 2025 consolidation incorporates
   **Regulation (EU) 2025/2088**, whose Article 7 amends AMLAR (Art 5(1)(k), Art 55(1), Art 88(3a), new
   Art 92a). That amending act is real and already integrated into
   [`docs/instruments/amla-2024-1620.md`](../instruments/amla-2024-1620.md), `data/documents.yaml`
   (`REG-2025-2088`) and `TIMELINE.md`. Nothing from #58 is to be reverted. The representation flapping is a
   plausible explanation for *extra spurious* T1-04 diffs, not for that one.

No content layer is affected: no new document, so `data/documents.yaml` and `sources/README.md` need no
