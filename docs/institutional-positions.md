# Implementation actors

> Post-adoption tracker: the co-legislators (Council, Parliament) concluded their role on adoption in May
> 2024. This page maps the **implementation actors** instead. One-screen current state: [`STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md).

| Actor | Role now | Headline position |
|---|---|---|
| **European Commission** (DG FISMA) | Adopts AMLA's draft RTS/ITS as delegated/implementing acts | Endorses the single-rulebook architecture; may set a lower (15% or below) UBO threshold for high-risk sectors |
| **AMLA** (Frankfurt) | Drafts the single rulebook (Level-2/3); future direct supervisor | Deliver 24 of 40 mandates in 2026, financial sector first; direct supervision from 2028 — [pipeline](amla-pipeline.md) |
| **EBA** | Legacy AML mandates transferred to AMLA (end-2025/Jan 2026) | Its AML guidelines remain valid until replaced (Art 54(5) AMLAR) |
| **ECB** | Prudential interface | Consulted on AML/CFT framework matters |
| **National supervisors** (NL: DNB, AFM, BFT) | Supervise non-selected entities to AMLA standards | DNB adopts the AMLA risk methodology from 2027 — [`netherlands.md`](netherlands.md) §e |
| **Member States** | Transpose AMLD6 | NL via the **Iwt** (at Raad van State, 24 Apr 2026) — [`netherlands.md`](netherlands.md) |

For the detailed live pipeline see [`amla-pipeline.md`](amla-pipeline.md); for the Dutch leg see
[`netherlands.md`](netherlands.md); contested issues in [`fault-lines.md`](fault-lines.md).
