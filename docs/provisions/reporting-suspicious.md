# Reporting & FIU powers

**Tracked because:** changes the reporting trigger (in the Netherlands) and tightens FIU response timelines;
directly operational for compliance functions.

## Change
- In the Netherlands, the reporting trigger moves from "**ongebruikelijke**" (unusual) transactions toward
  "**verdachte**" (suspicious) transactions under the new regime.
- The AMLR sets a **five-working-day deadline** for responding to FIU information requests.
- **FIU-Nederland gains broader powers**, including temporary transaction suspension (via AMLD6 / the Iwt).

## Netherlands
FIU powers are re-regulated by the [Iwt](../instruments/amld6-2024-1640.md) — see [`docs/netherlands.md`](../netherlands.md) §a.

## Status
**Adopted; applies 10 July 2027.** Operational impact on transaction-monitoring and FIU-response processes.
