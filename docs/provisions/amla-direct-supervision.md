# AMLA direct supervision

**Tracked because:** introduces an EU-level supervisor directly supervising selected high-risk groups — a
structural change to the supervisory landscape.

## New regime
- [AMLA](../instruments/amla-2024-1620.md) will select **~40 of the most complex, high-risk financial
  institutions/groups** for **direct supervision during 2027**, with direct supervision starting **2028** via
  **Joint Supervisory Teams**.
- All other entities remain with **national supervisors** (in NL: DNB, AFM, BFT) but to AMLA standards.
- Selection methodology: **RTS on selection of obliged entities (Art 12(7) AMLAR)**, finalised end-2025; the
  data-collection exercise to calibrate it began **March 2026**.
- Risk-profiling methodology: **RTS on inherent/residual risk profile (Art 40(2) AMLD6)**, Final Report
  16 Dec 2025, applies from 31 Dec 2027 — from 2027 DNB calculates institutions' risk scores using it.

## Status
**Selection during 2027; direct supervision from 2028** (planned/targeted dates). See [`amla-pipeline.md`](../amla-pipeline.md)
and [`docs/netherlands.md`](../netherlands.md) §e (DNB IRAP changes).
