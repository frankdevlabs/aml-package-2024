# Cash-payment limit

**Tracked because:** a hard operational rule already partly in force in the Netherlands, with an EU floor and a
member-state option to go lower.

## EU rule (AMLR)
- EU-wide cap on cash payments of **€10,000** for traders in goods/services (Art 80 AMLR).
- Member states may set a **lower** national cap (Art 80 member-state option).

## Netherlands
- **€3,000 cash-payment ban for goods** in force since **1 January 2026** under the Wet plan van aanpak
  witwassen (dossier 36.228; Staatsblad 2025, 362) — anchored in Art 1f Wwft.
- Applies to professional dealers in goods, pawnshops, art dealers. **Article IIA** (cash-*acceptance* duty
  under €3,000) **not yet in force** pending an exemptions decree.
- The €3,000 limit **extends to services from 10 July 2027** under the AMLR (NL exercising the Art 80
  member-state option below the EU €10,000 default).
- Detail + sources: [`docs/netherlands.md`](../netherlands.md) §b.

## Status
**Goods: in force (NL, 1 Jan 2026). Services: from 10 Jul 2027.** EU €10,000 cap applies from 10 Jul 2027.
