# Single rulebook & CDD harmonisation

**Tracked because:** the most operationally consequential shift for obliged entities — onboarding/KYC journeys
must be redesigned against directly-applicable, prescriptive CDD rules, and statutory references move from
Wwft/AMLD4 to the AMLR.

## Current regime (AMLD4 / Wwft)
A Directive transposed into national law (in NL, the Wwft), with material national divergence in CDD content
and procedures.

## AMLR regime
- The [AMLR](../instruments/amlr-2024-1624.md) is **directly applicable** and **revokes most of the Wwft on
  10 July 2027**; internal AML documentation must re-reference the AMLR (Osborne Clarke:
  https://www.osborneclarke.com/insights/key-implications-anti-money-laundering-regulation-amlr-financial-institutions-aml-policies).
- More prescriptive minimum content for internal policies, controls and procedures.
- The **Art 28(1) AMLR CDD RTS** (final draft due to the Commission by 10 July 2026, building on the EBA's
  30 October 2025 draft) sets the detailed CDD information/verification fields — likely requiring redesign of
  onboarding journeys and KYC data capture.
- Existing customer files are updated on a **risk-based** basis — no requirement to fully refresh all customers
  by July 2027.
