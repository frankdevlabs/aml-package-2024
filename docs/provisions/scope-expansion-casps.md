# Obliged-entity scope expansion

**Tracked because:** brings whole new sectors into AML/CFT obligations for the first time — most notably all
crypto-asset service providers and virtual IBANs.

## New obliged entities under the AMLR
- **All crypto-asset service providers (CASPs)** — dovetails with the recast [TFR (EU) 2023/1113](../instruments/tfr-2023-1113.md) "travel rule" (applicable since 30 Dec 2024).
- **Crowdfunding service providers / intermediaries.**
- **Mortgage / consumer-credit intermediaries** that are not financial institutions.
- **Professional football clubs and agents** (application deferred to **10 July 2029** — Art 3(3)(n)/(o), Art 90 AMLR).
- **High-value goods dealers.**
- **Virtual IBANs** are brought into scope for the first time (CSSF:
  https://www.cssf.lu/en/2024/06/the-new-aml-cft-regulation-the-sixth-aml-cft-directive-and-the-future-eu-aml-cft-supervisor/).

## Status
**Adopted; applies 10 July 2027** (football sub-set 10 July 2029). Newly-covered entities must build AML/CFT
programmes ahead of the application date.
