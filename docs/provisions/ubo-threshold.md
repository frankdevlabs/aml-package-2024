# UBO threshold — "25% or more"

**Tracked because:** widens the beneficial-owner population for every obliged entity and feeds the KvK
UBO-register; a small wording change with broad operational reach.

## Current law
Beneficial owner = a natural person holding "**more than 25%**" of shares/voting rights. An individual holding
exactly 25% is **not** currently a UBO.

## AMLR
- Threshold lowered to "**25% or more**" — **Art 52 AMLR** (covering both direct and indirect interests). The
  individual holding exactly 25% **becomes** a UBO. (Loyens & Loeff:
  https://www.loyensloeff.com/insights/news--events/news/new-eu-legislation-for-the-identification-and-registration-of-ubos/)
- The **Commission may set a lower threshold of 15% — or lower — for high-risk sectors**.
- AMLD6 strengthens the role of company registers (in NL, the KvK) and adds a **discrepancy-reporting duty**
  (within 14 days under the AMLR).

## Netherlands
Interacts with the KvK UBO-register. See [`docs/netherlands.md`](../netherlands.md) §c.

## Status
**Adopted; applies 10 July 2027.** Watch for a Commission delegated act setting a lower high-risk-sector threshold.
