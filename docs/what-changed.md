# What changed — full provision-by-provision diff

The comprehensive companion to [`STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md)'s "What changed" summary — the **authority on what
moved**. Because the package is adopted, each row traces **current law (AMLD4 / NL Wwft) → adopted AML Package
(in force; AMLR/AMLD6 apply 10 July 2027)**. The "moving" Level-2/3 detail is tracked separately in
[`amla-pipeline.md`](amla-pipeline.md) and [`extracts/amla/`](../extracts/amla/). Positions:
[`data/positions.csv`](../data/positions.csv).

| Provision | Current law (AMLD4 / Wwft) | Adopted AML Package | Level-2/3 detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Single rulebook / direct applicability**](provisions/cdd-harmonisation.md) | Directive transposed into the Wwft; national divergence | AMLR directly applicable; most of the Wwft revoked 10 Jul 2027 | — |

| [**UBO threshold**](provisions/ubo-threshold.md) | "more than 25%" | "25% or more" (Art 52); 14-day discrepancy reporting | Commission may set ≤15% for high-risk sectors |

| [**Ongoing monitoring**](provisions/cdd-harmonisation.md) | National practice | Harmonised ongoing-monitoring duty (Art 26) | [Art 26(5) guidelines](../extracts/amla/GL-ongoing-monitoring-art26-5_consultation-paper.md#sec-2) |

| [**Reporting (NL trigger)**](provisions/reporting-suspicious.md) | "ongebruikelijke" (unusual) | shift to "verdachte" (suspicious); FIU 5-working-day response deadline | — |
